Communities are where most of Hive3 happens: they publish events, courses, quests, jobs, content and posts, and the ones you join feed your home page. This page shows you how to find them and how to get in.

Anyone can browse. Joining, applying and subscribing need a Hive3 account, and the free Worker Bee plan is enough for all three. If you are signed out when you choose to join, Hive3 asks you to sign in or create an account first and then finishes the join for you.

## Find communities and accounts

Hive3 separates two directories. A **community** is an account on a paid plan, and people join it. Everyone else appears under **accounts**. Both have their own page, and Discover searches across both.

| Where | What you find |
|---|---|
| <UI>Discover</UI> | Search and browse communities, accounts, events, courses, quests and content in one place. |
| <UI>Explore Communities</UI> on the home page | The Communities directory, with a <UI>Search communities...</UI> box. |
| Accounts directory | People and other free accounts, with a <UI>Search accounts...</UI> box. From an empty home feed, select <UI>Find accounts</UI>. |

### Search with Discover

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Discover">
Select <UI>Discover</UI> in the sidebar. On a phone, use the Discover icon in the bottom bar.
</Step>
<Step title="Type what you are looking for">
Enter at least three characters in <UI>Search for anything...</UI>. Hive3 waits a moment after you stop typing, then shows a <UI>Searching</UI> label with your words and a <UI>Clear</UI> button. Fewer than three characters shows a hint instead of results.
</Step>
<Step title="Narrow the results">
Use the tabs <UI>All</UI>, <UI>Communities</UI>, <UI>Accounts</UI>, <UI>Events</UI>, <UI>Courses</UI>, <UI>Quests</UI> and <UI>Content</UI>. A tab with no matches is greyed out.
</Step>
<Step title="Filter by topic (optional)">
Select <UI>Filters</UI> to open **Filter Results**. Pick one or more topic badges, or type in <UI>Search badges...</UI> to find one. Your picks show next to <UI>Active Filters</UI>; <UI>Clear All</UI> removes them.
</Step>
<Step title="Open a result">
Select a card to open the account, event, course, quest or piece of content.
</Step>
</Steps>

Without a search, each tab lists items from that directory, and <UI>See all</UI> at the end of a tab opens the full directory for that type. Jobs are not in Discover; open <UI>Jobs</UI> in the sidebar. See [Find and apply for jobs](/explore/find-jobs).

## Join, apply or subscribe

Every account chooses how people get in. The button on its page tells you which kind you are looking at.

| The account is... | The button says | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Open | <UI>Join Community</UI> | You join at once. The button changes to <UI>Joined</UI>. |
| Private | <UI>Apply to Join</UI> | Your application goes to the account's admins. The button shows <UI>Pending Approval</UI> until they decide. |
| Open and paid | <UI>Subscribe</UI> | There is no approval step. You subscribe to the plan and get access to what it unlocks. |
| Private and paid | <UI>Apply to Join</UI>, then <UI>Subscribe</UI> | An admin approves you first. After approval the button becomes <UI>Subscribe</UI>, and <UI>Subscribed</UI> once you have paid. |

<Callout type="note">
Hive3 has no separate Follow button. Joining an open account is how you follow it: its posts then appear in your home feed, and it appears in <UI>My Hive</UI>.
</Callout>

An account's owner sees no join button on their own page.

### Join an open community

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the community's page">
Select its card in Discover or the Communities directory.
</Step>
<Step title="Select Join Community">
The button changes to <UI>Joined</UI>. A bell button appears beside it.
</Step>
</Steps>

To leave, point at <UI>Joined</UI>: it turns into <UI>Leave Community</UI>. Select it to leave.

<Steps>
<Step title="Select Apply to Join">
The button changes to <UI>Pending Approval</UI>. The account's admins are notified of your application.
</Step>
<Step title="Wait for a decision">
Come back to the community's page or <UI>My Hive</UI>. Once an admin approves you, the button shows <UI>Joined</UI> (or <UI>Subscribe</UI> if the community is also paid).
</Step>
</Steps>

Content that needs membership stays behind a **Private Content** panel until you are in. An event from a private community shows a prompt to join or apply first, with a <UI>View community</UI> button.

### Subscribe to a paid community

Select <UI>Subscribe</UI>, or open the <UI>Offerings</UI> tab and pick a plan. Payment, renewals and cancelling are covered in [Tickets and subscriptions](/explore/tickets-and-subscriptions).

## What membership gives you

- Posts from the community appear in **The Buzz**, including posts marked <UI>Members only</UI>. See [Posts and the Buzz](/explore/the-buzz).
- Events, courses and content that the community restricts to members open up.
- If the community is on a paid plan, a shared chat for its members appears in [Messages](/explore/messages).
- A bell button next to <UI>Joined</UI> turns notifications from that community on or off. Its tooltip reads "Notifications on for this account" or "Notifications off for this account".

## The tabs on an account page

Every account page has <UI>Overview</UI>, <UI>Activity</UI>, <UI>Communities</UI>, <UI>Credentials</UI> and <UI>Affiliates</UI>. These appear when the account has something to show: <UI>Offerings</UI>, <UI>Events</UI>, <UI>Metaverse</UI>, <UI>Content</UI>, <UI>Courses</UI>, <UI>Jobs</UI>, <UI>NFTs</UI>, <UI>Quests</UI> and <UI>Streams</UI>.

## See what you have joined

<Path items={["My Hive", "Communities"]} />

<UI>My Hive</UI> lists the communities you have joined on its <UI>Communities</UI> tab, and free accounts you have joined on its <UI>Accounts</UI> tab. The other tabs gather your events, courses, quests and job applications.
