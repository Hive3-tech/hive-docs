A course is a series of lessons you can take at your own pace, with progress tracked for you. Some courses also run office hours, and some award a credential when you finish.

Any account can take courses, including Worker Bee. Some courses ask you to subscribe to the creator or buy a ticket first.

## Find a course

Select <UI>Courses</UI> in the sidebar. Type in <UI>Search courses...</UI> or use the filter menu: <UI>All courses</UI>, <UI>Beginner</UI>, <UI>Intermediate</UI> or <UI>Advanced</UI>. A **Featured Courses** row highlights courses picked by Hive3. Courses you are enrolled in also appear under <Path items={["My Hive", "Courses"]} />.

## Enroll

Open a course. The page shows its title, creator, number of lessons and level, then the description and, on a computer, the list of lessons on the right.

<Steps>
<Step title="Select the enroll button">
The label tells you how enrollment works:
- <UI>Enroll Now</UI> enrolls you immediately.
- <UI>Apply to Enroll</UI> sends an application. The button becomes <UI>Application Pending</UI>, with a <UI>Cancel Application</UI> option, until the creator decides.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm you are in">
Once you are enrolled, the button reads <UI>Enrolled</UI> and a **Course Progress** bar appears. You are notified when you enroll, and again when a creator accepts or declines an application.
</Step>
</Steps>

Until you are enrolled, the lessons list shows **Enroll to Access Lessons**.

If the course needs a ticket or a subscription, an **Access Required** panel with <UI>Buy Ticket</UI> and <UI>Subscribe</UI> replaces the enroll button. Pay first, see a **You have access** badge, then enroll. See [Tickets and subscriptions](/explore/tickets-and-subscriptions).

### Leave a course

Point at <UI>Enrolled</UI>: it turns into <UI>Quit Course</UI>. Select it and confirm. Hive3 warns **Your course progress will be lost if not completed.**

## Work through the lessons

<Steps>
<Step title="Open a lesson">
Select <UI>Open</UI> next to a lesson. Each row shows the lesson number, and a green check once you have completed it.
</Step>
<Step title="Read the overview">
The first screen, **Overview**, introduces the lesson. Select <UI>Begin Lesson</UI> to start.
</Step>
<Step title="Move through the sections">
Lessons are split into sections: text, video and documents. A **Section 1 of 4** style label shows where you are, and the **Sections** list jumps to any section. Select the <UI>Next</UI> button, which names the next section, or <UI>Previous</UI> to go back.
</Step>
<Step title="Complete the lesson">
On the last section the button reads <UI>Complete Lesson</UI>. A <UI>Congrats!</UI> window offers <UI>Next Lesson</UI> and <UI>Back to Course</UI>.
</Step>
</Steps>

Text sections have controls to make the text smaller or larger and to enter fullscreen. Video sections play inside the page, and PDF sections open in a built-in viewer. After you complete a lesson, **Great Job!** appears with <UI>Restart</UI>, <UI>Back to Course Overview</UI> and <UI>Next Lesson</UI>.

<Callout type="tip">
A video hosted on YouTube or Vimeo first shows **External YouTube content is blocked** (or Vimeo). Select <UI>Load YouTube once</UI> to play it this time, or use <UI>Review storage choices</UI> to allow external media for good.
</Callout>

<Callout type="note">
Your progress is saved to your account, so you can leave and come back. On the course page, the bar reads **(n) of (total) lessons completed**.
</Callout>

## Join office hours

Some courses list **Office Hours**: scheduled sessions where the creator is available. The card shows whether they are virtual or in a 3D world, the days, the hours and the time zone.

While a session is live, <UI>Enter office hours</UI> appears. For a virtual session it opens the creator's link in a new tab. For a 3D-world session it opens the world's entry page (see [Attend events](/explore/attend-events#in-a-3d-world)). You must be enrolled and accepted in the course to enter a world session. If the link is not ready, the button reads <UI>Link not yet available</UI>.

## Earn the credential

If the creator attached a credential, a **Credential** card on the course page shows it. When you complete the final lesson, the <UI>Congrats!</UI> window offers <UI>Claim Credential</UI>, and you are notified that you completed the course.

You can claim later from <Path items={["Notifications", "Claim Credentials"]} />. Claiming asks you to use a wallet; see [Your credentials](/account/credentials).
