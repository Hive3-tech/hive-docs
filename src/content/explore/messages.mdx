Messages is where private conversations and community chats live. You can message any account directly, and every paid community you have joined has a shared chat for its members.

Everyone can read and reply to messages. Starting a brand-new direct message needs <PlanBadge tier="honey" />. If you are on Worker Bee, you can still reply to anyone who messages you.

## Open Messages

Select the Chats icon in the sidebar's quick actions, or <UI>Messages</UI> in the bottom bar on a phone. The list shows your conversations, newest first, with an unread count on each. Three buttons sit above it: <UI>New Message</UI>, <UI>Unknown</UI> and <UI>Archived</UI>.

## Start a conversation

<Callout type="plan">
Starting a direct message needs a paid plan (<PlanBadge tier="honey" />). On Worker Bee, selecting <UI>New Message</UI> opens **Upgrade Required**: "You need at least a Honey Bee membership to create new messages. Note: You can reply to anyone messaging you." Select <UI>View Plans</UI> to compare plans.
</Callout>

<Steps>
<Step title="Select New Message">
The **New Message** window opens.
</Step>
<Step title="Search for an account">
Type in <UI>Search for accounts...</UI> and pick the person or community from the results.
</Step>
<Step title="Check the warning (if shown)">
If neither of you has joined the other, Hive3 shows **Not Collaborating**: "Your message will be displayed in their Unknown inbox and they will have to accept it first." Select <UI>Continue</UI> to go ahead, which sends a 👋 to open the conversation, or <UI>Cancel</UI>.
</Step>
<Step title="Write your message">
Type in <UI>Type a message...</UI>. Press Enter to send, or Shift+Enter for a new line.
</Step>
</Steps>

A message can hold up to 4,000 characters. Use the emoji button to add emoji and <UI>Attach image</UI> to send a picture; a small note shows **Image ready to send** before you send it.

### Work with a message

Open the three-dot menu next to a message (<UI>Message actions</UI>, or <UI>Own message actions</UI> on yours):

- <UI>Reply</UI> quotes the message in your next one. Select the X on the quote (<UI>Cancel reply</UI>) to drop it.
- <UI>React</UI> opens an emoji picker. Reactions appear under the message with a count; select one to add or remove yours.
- <UI>Edit</UI> and <UI>Delete</UI> are available on your own messages. An edited message shows **(edited)**.

## Messages from people you do not know

A message from someone whose conversation you have not accepted goes to the **Unknown** inbox instead of your main list. A red count on <UI>Unknown</UI> shows how many are waiting.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Unknown">
Select <UI>Unknown</UI>. The **Unknown Messages** list appears, with a warning mark on each conversation.
</Step>
<Step title="Open a conversation">
A window titled **Unknown User** warns you to be careful with personal information and asks "Do you accept the risk?"
</Step>
<Step title="Choose">
Select <UI>Accept Risk</UI> to read the conversation and move it to your main list, or <UI>Cancel</UI> to leave it unopened.
</Step>
</Steps>

Until you accept, the sender can send up to 5 messages. You can archive a conversation from the Unknown list without opening it.

## Archive and mute

Open <UI>Chat options</UI> (three dots) at the top of a conversation:

- **Notifications on** / **Notifications off** switches notifications for that conversation.
- <UI>Archive Chat</UI> removes it from your main list.

You can also select the archive icon next to a conversation in the list. Select <UI>Archived</UI> to see archived chats, and the unarchive icon to bring one back. Opening an archived conversation also restores it.

## Community chats

For every community on a paid plan that you have joined, an **Account Channel** conversation appears in your list. It is a shared chat for the community's members. You can reply, react and attach images the same way as in a direct message. Moderators can delete other people's messages in the channel.

If the community connects Discord or Telegram, messages from those apps appear in the same chat with a small marker showing where they came from.

<Callout type="note">
You get a **New direct message** notification when someone writes to you. To pause alerts for one conversation, use **Notifications off** in its menu. See [Notifications](/explore/notifications).
</Callout>
