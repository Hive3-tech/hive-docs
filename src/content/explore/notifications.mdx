Notifications tell you when something needs your attention: a reply, a mention, an approval, a credential ready to claim. The same page also holds your credentials to claim and your ticket history.

Notifications are free on every plan.

## Open your notifications

Select the bell in the sidebar's quick actions. A number on the bell shows how many are unread. On a phone, open the menu from your photo in the bottom bar and select <UI>Notifications</UI>.

## Read and filter

Each notification shows an icon for its kind, a title, a short message and how long ago it arrived. A coloured dot marks the ones you have not read.

- Select a notification to mark it as read and open what it is about, such as the event, course, quest, job application or post.
- The tabs <UI>All</UI>, <UI>Unread</UI> and <UI>Read</UI> show a count each. <UI>Unread</UI> shows only the ones you have not opened.
- <UI>Mark all as read</UI> clears every unread dot at once.
- Point at a notification and select the trash icon (<UI>Delete notification</UI>) to remove it.

Hive3 keeps notifications for 90 days.

### What you are notified about

| Area | Examples |
|---|---|
| Posts | Someone likes or comments on your post, or mentions you. |
| Messages | A new direct message. |
| Communities | Someone joins your community or applies to it, and affiliate requests. |
| Events, courses and quests | Your RSVP or application, an organizer's decision, attendance recorded, and a course or quest completed. |
| Jobs | You applied, or an employer changed your application's status. |
| Tickets and money | A ticket purchase, a refund request and its outcome, and a reminder when a Hive3 membership payment is due. |
| Credentials | A credential is ready to claim or has been awarded. |
| Help | Updates on a report or suggestion you sent. |

## Claim credentials

Credentials you have earned from events, courses and quests, and from a Camera Liveness Check, wait here until you claim them.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the Claim Credentials panel">
On a computer it is in the right-hand column. On a phone, select the <UI>Claim Credentials</UI> tile at the top of the page. A number shows how many are waiting.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick a credential">
Each row shows its name and where it came from: <UI>Event</UI>, <UI>Course</UI>, <UI>Quest</UI>, <UI>Camera Liveness Check</UI> or <UI>General</UI>. Select <UI>Claim</UI>.
</Step>
<Step title="Connect a wallet">
The credential opens in a window. If you have no wallet yet, choose <UI>Create my Hive3 wallet</UI> (protected by a passkey on your device) or <UI>Connect my own wallet</UI>. A <UI>Small claim fee</UI> badge tells you a small network fee applies, and the window shows how to add the amount if your wallet needs funding.
</Step>
<Step title="Finish the claim">
Follow the prompts in the window. When it succeeds, the credential shows that you have already minted it, with a link to the transaction.
</Step>
</Steps>

If a credential is not ready yet, the window says why. For example, claiming opens when the event or course starts. See [Your credentials](/account/credentials) and [Wallets](/account/wallets).

## My Tickets

<UI>My Tickets</UI> is your purchase history for tickets. On a computer it is a panel in the right-hand column. On a phone, select the <UI>My Tickets</UI> tile at the top of the page.

Each ticket shows what it is for, a label for the type (event, course or quest), when you bought it and the price you paid. A badge shows <UI>Refund pending</UI>, <UI>Refund rejected</UI> or <UI>Refunded</UI> when a refund is in progress or done. Select a ticket to open the event, course or quest it unlocks. To ask for money back, see [Tickets and subscriptions](/explore/tickets-and-subscriptions#refunds).

## Choose what you get alerted about

You have three controls.

| Control | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Per account | The bell next to <UI>Joined</UI> on an account's page | Turns notifications from that account on or off. |
| Per conversation | <UI>Chat options</UI> in Messages | Turns notifications for one conversation on or off. |
| Everything | <Path items={["Settings", "Display & notifications"]} /> | The <UI>System notifications</UI> switch stops new notifications for your account. |

The same settings page has a **Community newsletters by email** switch for email from communities you have joined. You can also unsubscribe from one community in any of its emails.

<Callout type="note">
Turning off <UI>System notifications</UI> does not hide ticket refund decisions. You are still told when a refund request is approved or rejected.
</Callout>
