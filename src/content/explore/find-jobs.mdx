Communities and creators post jobs on Hive3 so they can hire from the people who already follow their work. You browse, apply with a resume and an optional cover letter, and the employer reviews your application.

Any account can apply, including Worker Bee. Some jobs are only visible to subscribers of the employer.

## Browse jobs

Select <UI>Jobs</UI> in the sidebar. The page, **Jobs**, is described as "Discover opportunities in Web3 and beyond", and shows how many jobs match.

- Type in <UI>Search jobs by title, company, or keyword...</UI> to search by job title or employer name.
- Select <UI>Filters</UI> to open **Filter Jobs**. Pick tags such as <UI>Full-time</UI>, <UI>Contract</UI>, <UI>Internship</UI>, <UI>Remote</UI>, <UI>Senior</UI> or <UI>Web3</UI>, or type in <UI>Search filters...</UI> to find one. Your picks show as <UI>Active Filters</UI>, and <UI>Clear All</UI> resets them.

Each card shows the job title, the employer, the type of job, the location, the pay range if one is given, and when it was posted. Select a card to open the job. The list loads 50 jobs at a time; use <UI>Previous</UI> and <UI>Next</UI> at the bottom to move between pages.

## Read a job

A job page shows the title, employer (select it to open their account), where the job is and what type it is, the tags, and a **Job Description**. A **Compensation** figure appears when the employer set a pay range.

If the employer limits jobs to subscribers, the description is blurred and a panel reads **(Employer) Subscription Required**. Select <UI>View Subscription Options</UI> to see how to get access. See [Tickets and subscriptions](/explore/tickets-and-subscriptions).

## Apply for a job

<Steps>
<Step title="Select Apply for Job">
You need to be signed in. Job managers do not see this button on their own listings.
</Step>
<Step title="Write a cover letter (optional)">
In the **Apply for Job** window, type in the <UI>Cover Letter</UI> box. A counter shows how many characters you have written.
</Step>
<Step title="Upload your resume">
Select **Upload your resume** and choose a PDF up to 25 MB. This step is required: <UI>Submit Application</UI> stays unavailable until a resume is attached. Use the X next to the file to replace it.
</Step>
<Step title="Submit">
Select <UI>Submit Application</UI>. Your application goes directly to the employer.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Figure
  src="/media/explore/find-jobs/apply-modal.webp"

After you apply, the job page shows **Applied** and "You have already applied for this position". You cannot apply twice to the same job while your application is active.

## Follow your applications

Jobs you have applied to appear under <Path items={["My Hive", "Jobs"]} />.

The employer moves your application through four states: pending, reviewed, accepted or rejected. Each change sends you a notification such as **Your application for (job) is now accepted.** Open <UI>Notifications</UI> to see it. See [Notifications](/explore/notifications).

## Reapply after a rejection

If an employer rejects your application, the job page offers <UI>Apply for Job</UI> again.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the job and select Apply for Job">
The window is titled **Reapply for Job** and explains that your previous application was rejected and that submitting again creates a new application.
</Step>
<Step title="Update your documents">
Add a cover letter if you like and upload a resume. You can use a different one from last time.
</Step>
<Step title="Select Submit Reapplication">
The employer is notified that you reapplied.
</Step>
</Steps>
