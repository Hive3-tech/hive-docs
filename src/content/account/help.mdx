## Send a report

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose the kind">
In the <UI>Help & Feedback</UI> card, select <UI>Report a bug</UI> or <UI>Make a suggestion</UI>. You can switch between <UI>Bug report</UI> and <UI>Suggestion</UI> on the next page.
</Step>
<Step title="Describe it">
Write in <UI>Description</UI>, up to 5,000 characters. For a bug, say what happened, what you expected and the steps to reproduce it. For a suggestion, say what you would like Hive3 to do and why.
</Step>
<Step title="Attach files (optional)">
Add up to 4 images (PNG, JPG, GIF or WebP, 8 MB each) and 1 video (MP4, WebM or MOV, 19 MB).
</Step>
<Step title="Submit">
Select <UI>Submit bug report</UI> or <UI>Submit suggestion</UI>.
</Step>
</Steps>

You see <UI>Ticket</UI> and your ticket number straight away, with a <UI>Copy</UI> button. If your account has a verified email, Hive3 sends a confirmation with the number. Otherwise keep the number somewhere safe. It also appears in your notifications unless you have muted system notifications.

## Follow your tickets

Select <UI>View your tickets</UI> to see everything you have submitted. Open a ticket to see your report, its status (Open, In progress, Resolved or Closed) and the conversation with the team. You can reply in the conversation while the ticket is open. Once a ticket is closed you cannot reply, so submit a new report if the issue comes back.

## Developer API keys

The same section has a <UI>Developer API keys</UI> card. Select <UI>Manage API keys</UI> to create and revoke keys. See [Authentication and API keys](/developers/api-authentication).
