## Open Settings

Choose **Settings** in one of these places:

- At the bottom of the sidebar.
- In your account menu, at the top of the sidebar.
- On your dashboard, in the <UI>Account Settings</UI> card.

The page is headed <UI>Settings</UI>, with the line "Your account, plan, wallets and preferences."

On a computer, the list of sections sits on the left and the open section on the right, starting with <UI>Account</UI>. On a phone, Settings opens as a list. Select a section to open it, then select <UI>All settings</UI> to come back to the list.

## Account

The <UI>Account</UI> section is the longest. From top to bottom it holds:

- **Handle.** Your @handle is part of your profile link. Type a new one and select <UI>Save handle</UI>. Hive3 checks that it is available first. Changing it breaks links that use the old one, so update anywhere you have shared it.
- **Email.** Shows your address and whether it is verified. If it is not, select <UI>Verify email</UI>.
- **Sign-in methods.** The accounts you can use to sign in: Google, GitHub, X and Discord. A linked method shows <UI>Linked</UI>; an unlinked one has a <UI>Connect</UI> button. Apple and LinkedIn are marked as coming soon.
- **Security.** Passkeys and 2-step verification. See [Sign in and security](/account/security).
- **Camera Liveness Check** and **Proof of human.** See [Liveness and proof of human](/account/proof-of-human).

## Subscriptions

The <UI>Subscriptions</UI> section has two cards.

- <UI>Hive3 Membership</UI> shows your current plan and its status. Select <UI>Change plan</UI> to compare plans, or <UI>Manage billing in Stripe</UI> to update your card or cancel. If you are on Worker Bee, the card offers <UI>Start membership</UI>.
- <UI>Community & People Subscriptions</UI> lists subscriptions you bought from creators and communities, with a <UI>Manage billing in Stripe</UI> button for each. If one has expired, the button reads <UI>Subscribe again</UI>.

Cancellations made in Stripe appear in this list within about a minute.

## Linked communities

<UI>Linked communities</UI> lists communities that are linked to your account through consent you gave earlier. Each entry shows when it was linked. Select <UI>Disconnect</UI> and confirm to end the link. The community can no longer manage your subscription or profile on your behalf, and you stay a member of it.

## Display & notifications

This section controls what you see and what you are sent.

- **Theme.** Choose <UI>light</UI>, <UI>dark</UI> or <UI>system</UI>. System follows your device.
- **System notifications.** A switch that turns Hive3 notifications on or off.
- **Community newsletters by email.** A switch for emails from the communities you have joined. Communities can only email you when this is on and your email is verified. Each email also has a link to unsubscribe from that one community. See [Email broadcasts](/community/broadcasts).
