Hive3 offers three optional badges you can show on your profile: the Camera Liveness Check, World ID and Human ID. They work differently and make different claims, so this page keeps them apart.

## Camera Liveness Check

The Camera Liveness Check uses your camera to run a short, on-device check that a live face is in front of the screen. Hive3 describes it as a **client-unattested** check. It is a local check that runs in your browser.

<Callout type="warning">
This is not KYC or identity verification. It is a client-unattested completion, not KYC, identity, uniqueness, personhood, Sybil-resistance or adversarial-liveness verification. It creates no permanent credential and grants no consequential access.
</Callout>

### What stays on your device

- Camera frames and landmark progress remain local, in memory and volatile. They are cleared when the attempt ends.
- Hive3's server stores only account-linked method, policy, challenge, completion and expiry metadata. It receives no image, frame, landmark, descriptor or movement telemetry.

### Run the check

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the card">
In <UI>Account</UI> settings, find the <UI>Camera Liveness Check</UI> card. It tells you whether you have a current completion. If you have none, it reads "No current Camera Liveness completion."
</Step>
<Step title="Start">
Select <UI>Start Camera Liveness Check</UI>. A window titled <UI>Before you start the Camera Liveness Check</UI> shows the notice above.
</Step>
<Step title="Acknowledge the notice">
Tick the box that begins "I understand this is a client-unattested completion", then select <UI>Acknowledge and continue</UI>. You cannot continue until you tick it.
</Step>
<Step title="Allow the camera">
Your browser asks to use your camera. Allow it. Hive3 loads its face detector on your device and starts the check.
</Step>
<Step title="Follow the prompts">
The window, titled <UI>Camera Liveness Check</UI>, shows a cue for each step. When it asks, select <UI>Continue to blink</UI> and blink. If blink detection struggles, reduce glare or briefly take off your glasses for that step.
</Step>
</Steps>

### After the check

The card reads "Client-asserted camera check completed", with the date you completed it and the date it expires, and a <UI>Current</UI> badge. While the check is current, a <UI>Camera Liveness Check</UI> badge can appear on your profile.

A completion lasts 90 days. In the last 14 days the badge changes to <UI>Expiring</UI> and the button becomes <UI>Renew Camera Liveness Check</UI>. If it expires, the card says "Expired — complete a new check."

If the check does not finish, the card says <UI>Camera Liveness Check did not complete</UI> and offers <UI>Retry Camera Liveness Check</UI>. If the card says the check is temporarily unavailable, try again later.

## World ID

World ID is a separate service, run by World, that you link to your Hive3 account. A linked World ID shows a badge such as <UI>World ID · Orb</UI> on your profile. Hive3 receives a zero-knowledge proof, not your identity.

| Level | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Orb** | World's Orb verification. World describes it as showing that you are a unique human. |
| **Selfie Check** | A lighter liveness check from World. It does not guarantee one person per account. |

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the World ID row">
Under <UI>Proof of human</UI>, find <UI>World ID</UI>.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose a level">
Select <UI>Verify with Orb</UI>, or <UI>Use Selfie Check</UI> for the lighter check.
</Step>
<Step title="Approve in World App">
Follow the World ID window and approve the request in World App. Make sure World App is up to date, because Hive3 accepts World ID 4.0 credentials only.
</Step>
</Steps>

A message confirms <UI>World ID linked to your Hive3 account</UI>. If you linked with Selfie Check and later verify at an Orb, select <UI>Upgrade to Orb</UI>. Select <UI>Unlink</UI> to remove it. If the row says World ID linking is not available yet, it is not turned on for your account yet.

## Human ID

Human ID, from human.tech, is a verification you complete once with a face check or a government ID. Hive3 looks for it on your linked wallet. A linked Human ID shows a badge such as <UI>Human ID · Biometrics</UI>.

<Steps>
<Step title="Verify with Human ID (if you have not)">
Use <UI>Verify with biometrics</UI> or <UI>Verify with government ID</UI> in the row. These open Human ID's own site, where you verify with your wallet.
</Step>
<Step title="Check from Hive3">
Back in Settings, select <UI>Already verified?</UI>. If you have no wallet linked yet, Hive3 asks you to connect the wallet you verified with. Otherwise it looks on the wallets you have already linked.
</Step>
<Step title="Link it">
When Hive3 finds a verification, it links it and shows how long each method is valid. If it finds nothing, the row offers <UI>Connect that wallet</UI> in case you verified with a different wallet.
</Step>
</Steps>

Select <UI>Check again</UI> to refresh a linked verification, or <UI>Unlink</UI> to remove it. If you verified before you joined, Hive3 may find and link it automatically the first time you open the row.

## Good to know

- A World ID or a Human ID wallet can back only one Hive3 account at a time. Unlinking frees it.
- Hive3 stores which provider you linked, when, and the provider's anonymous identifier so it cannot be linked to a second account. Unlinking removes it.
- The three badges are independent. A Camera Liveness Check badge does not mean you have a World ID or Human ID, and the other way round.
- Each of these is optional. Hive3 describes World ID and Human ID as optional links that show a badge on your profile.
