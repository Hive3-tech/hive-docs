## Privacy settings

The <UI>Privacy settings</UI> card lets you update your preferences for analytics, marketing and external media at any time. Select <UI>Update privacy settings</UI> to reopen your choices. Hive3 states that it does not sell personal information.

The card also links to the <UI>Privacy Policy</UI>, the <UI>Cookie and Device Storage Policy</UI>, the <UI>Terms & Conditions</UI> and the <UI>Acceptable Use Policy</UI>.

## Export your data

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Manage Account">
In <UI>Privacy & data</UI>, find the <UI>Manage Account</UI> card.
</Step>
<Step title="Export">
Select <UI>Export Data</UI>. Hive3 prepares a ZIP file and your browser downloads it.
</Step>
</Steps>

The ZIP has a manifest, your profile and account details, and CSV files for items such as sign-in methods, linked wallets, preferences, follows, memberships, notifications, feedback tickets and consent records. If you export again soon after, Hive3 may return your recent export instead of building a new one.

## Delete your account

<Callout type="warning">
Deleting your account hides it right away. You have seven days to change your mind.
</Callout>

<Steps>
<Step title="Start deletion">
In <UI>Manage Account</UI>, select <UI>Delete Account</UI>.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm">
The <UI>Delete your account?</UI> window explains that your public account is hidden immediately. Type `DELETE` and select <UI>Delete Account</UI>. You are signed out.
</Step>
</Steps>

### During the seven days

- Your public profile and account content are hidden.
- Requesting deletion also cancels the subscriptions and plan billing tied to your account.
- When you sign in, you see <UI>Account Deletion Scheduled</UI> with the time remaining. Select <UI>Stop deletion</UI> to restore your account, or <UI>Continue deletion</UI> to keep it scheduled.

After the seven days, the account is deleted.

Consider exporting your data first.
