A credential is a digital badge for something you did or were recognized for, such as attending an event, finishing a course, completing a quest or being awarded one by a community. You claim it to a wallet so it is yours to keep.

## Find a credential to claim

<Path items={["Notifications", "Claim Credentials"]} />

Open <UI>Notifications</UI> and choose <UI>Claim Credentials</UI>. The list shows credentials you are eligible to mint. When a community awards you one, you also get a notification that reads "You've been awarded a credential". Open it to go to the credential's page.

## Claim it

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the credential">
Select it in the list or from the notification. The page shows its image, description, attributes and network.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick a wallet">
If you have no wallet yet, the claim panel offers <UI>Create my Hive3 wallet</UI>, secured by a passkey on your device, or <UI>Connect my own wallet</UI>. On networks where Hive3 wallets are not available, only your own wallet is offered. See [Wallets](/account/wallets).
</Step>
<Step title="Fund the claim if asked">
Some networks need a small amount of the network's currency. The panel then shows the amount and your wallet address. Use <UI>Copy address</UI> to add funds, then <UI>Check again</UI>.
</Step>
<Step title="Mint">
Select <UI>Mint Credential</UI>. While it is processing the button reads <UI>Claim in progress</UI>. A claimed credential shows <UI>Already Minted</UI>.
</Step>
</Steps>

A credential cannot be claimed until its setup on the network is confirmed. If the button reads <UI>Not Yet Deployed</UI>, or the page says claiming opens after setup, try again later. <UI>Not Eligible</UI> means this credential is not available to your account.

## See your credentials

Claimed credentials appear in the <UI>Credentials</UI> tab of your account page.
