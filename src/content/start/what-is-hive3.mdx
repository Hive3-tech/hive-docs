Hive3 brings communities, events, courses, quests, jobs and live streams together under one sign-in. You can use it to take part in what other people run, to run your own community, or both.

This page explains the ideas you will meet in the rest of the documentation. If you would rather start clicking, go straight to [Create your account](/start/create-your-account).

## What you can do

Everyone who signs in can:

- Join communities and follow accounts. See [Discover and join communities](/explore/discover-and-join).
- Attend events, take courses and complete quests. See [Attend events](/explore/attend-events), [Take courses](/explore/take-courses) and [Complete quests](/explore/complete-quests).
- Read and watch content, and find and apply for jobs.
- Post to The Buzz, the home feed, and send messages.
- Create events, courses, content, jobs, quests, add-ons and live streams of your own. See [Your dashboard](/create/your-dashboard).

A paid plan adds the tools for running a community: private and paid access, admins and moderators, email broadcasts, an AI assistant and more. [Plans and pricing](/start/plans) lists every difference.

## Accounts and communities

Everything in Hive3 belongs to an **account**. When you sign in you get an account with your display name, handle and profile page.

A **community** is an account that is on a paid plan. Hive3 lists paid accounts on the <UI>Communities</UI> page and Worker Bee accounts on the <UI>Accounts</UI> page, so an account moves from one list to the other when its plan changes. There is no separate community product to buy: you upgrade the account you already have.

<Figure src="/media/start/what-is-hive3/communities-directory.webp" alt="The Communities page listing paid accounts as cards with a search field" caption="The Communities page lists paid accounts." />

You can link more than one sign-in to your browser and move between them. See [Switch between accounts](/start/find-your-way-around#switch-between-accounts).

## Members and roles

People take part in a community by becoming members. How someone joins depends on how the community is set up:

| Button on the community page | What it means |
|---|---|
| <UI>Join Community</UI> | Anyone can join straight away. |
| <UI>Apply to Join</UI> | The community reviews applications before accepting you. |
| <UI>Subscribe</UI> | Access needs a paid subscription to that community. |

Joined communities appear in <UI>My Hive</UI>, on the <UI>Communities</UI> tab.

Running a community uses three roles above regular member: **Owner**, **Admin** and **Moderator**. Anyone with one of these roles can open the community's [dashboard](/create/your-dashboard). Admins can give other members the Admin or Moderator role, which needs the community to be on a paid plan. See [Roles and permissions](/community/roles-and-permissions).

## Plans

Hive3 has four plans: Worker Bee (free), Honey Bee, Swarm and Royal Hive. The plan belongs to the account, not to the person. Worker Bee already lets you create events, courses, content, jobs, quests, add-ons and live streams. Paid plans add privacy, selling, community management tools and monthly allowances. Compare them on [Plans and pricing](/start/plans).

## The dashboard

Your **dashboard** is where you publish and manage. It has a tab for each kind of thing you create (<UI>Events</UI>, <UI>Courses</UI>, <UI>Content</UI>, <UI>Jobs</UI>, <UI>Quests</UI>), plus action cards for settings, members, broadcasts and more.

Open yours from <UI>My Hive</UI> by selecting <UI>My Dashboard</UI>. See [Your dashboard](/create/your-dashboard).

<Figure src="/media/start/what-is-hive3/dashboard.webp" alt="A personal account dashboard with action cards for Account Settings, Wallets, Accounts you manage, Account Builder, Streaming, Affiliates, Add Ons and Members, tabs for Events, Courses, Content, Jobs, Quests and Broadcasts, and summaries for Metaverse time and Quest credits" caption="A dashboard." />

## Acting as an account you manage

If you are an Owner, Admin or Moderator of another account, you can open that account's dashboard and publish as it. Anything you create there belongs to that account, and paid features follow that account's plan, not yours.

Select <UI>Accounts you manage</UI> on your dashboard to see them, then open the account's page and select the gear button labelled <UI>Dashboard</UI> in its header.
