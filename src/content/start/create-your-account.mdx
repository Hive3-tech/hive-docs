You create a Hive3 account the first time you sign in. There is no separate sign-up form: pick a sign-in method, then complete a short profile and you are in.

## Choose a sign-in method

Go to [app.hive3.tech](https://app.hive3.tech) and select <UI>Sign In</UI>. The sign-in page, titled **Sign in to Hive3**, offers these options.

<Figure src="/media/start/create-your-account/sign-in.webp" alt="The Hive3 sign-in page with an email field, buttons for Google, GitHub, Apple, X, Discord and LinkedIn, and buttons for Connect Wallet and Sign in with passkey" caption="The sign-in page. Apple and LinkedIn are marked Coming soon." />

| Method | How it works |
|---|---|
| Email magic link | Enter your email address and select <UI>Submit</UI>. Hive3 emails you a link; open it to sign in. No password is needed. |
| <UI>Google</UI>, <UI>GitHub</UI>, <UI>X</UI>, <UI>Discord</UI> | Approve Hive3 on the provider's own page and you return signed in. |
| <UI>Connect Wallet</UI> | Choose a wallet in the connection window, then approve the sign-in message in your wallet. |
| <UI>Sign in with passkey</UI> | Use a passkey you added earlier. This is for returning to an account, not for creating one. |

<UI>Apple</UI> and <UI>LinkedIn</UI> are shown on the page with a <UI>Coming soon</UI> label and cannot be used yet.

<Callout type="tip">
After you sign in, you can connect Google, GitHub, X or Discord to the same account under <Path items={["Settings", "Account"]} />. Select <UI>Connect</UI> next to a provider, and any linked method signs you in to the account. See [Sign-in and security](/account/security).
</Callout>

### Sign in with an email magic link

<Steps>
<Step title="Enter your email">
Type your address into the <UI>Enter email address</UI> field. The <UI>Submit</UI> button appears once the address contains an @.
</Step>
<Step title="Check your inbox">
Hive3 shows <UI>Check your email</UI>. If you typed the wrong address, select <UI>Change email</UI>.
</Step>
<Step title="Open the link">
Open the email and select the link. It signs you in and takes you to Hive3.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Add a passkey for later

<UI>Sign in with passkey</UI> only works once a passkey has been added to your account. After your first sign-in, add one under <Path items={["Settings", "Account"]} />. A wallet passkey created for your Hive3 wallet cannot be used to sign in.

## Complete your profile

The first time you sign in, Hive3 opens **Create your profile**. Everything on this page can be changed later.

<Figure src="/media/start/create-your-account/onboarding.webp" alt="The Create your profile page with a banner upload area, a profile photo upload, and fields for display name, handle and referral code, and an Enter Hive button" caption="Create your profile." />

<Steps>
<Step title="Add a banner and photo (optional)">
Select <UI>Click to upload banner</UI> for a wide banner image (1500 × 500 px works best) and choose a square profile photo (400 × 400 px).
</Step>
<Step title="Enter your display name">
<UI>Display name</UI> is required. It is the name people see on your profile and posts.
</Step>
<Step title="Check your handle">
Hive3 picks a <UI>Handle</UI> for you. Keep it or type another one. It needs at least three characters and must be available; a green check confirms it. Your handle is how people find and mention you.
</Step>
<Step title="Add a referral code (optional)">
If someone invited you, enter their code in <UI>Referral code</UI>. See [Referrals](/account/referrals).
</Step>
<Step title="Select Enter Hive">
<UI>Enter Hive</UI> becomes available once your display name and handle are valid. Hive3 saves your profile and sends you to the page you were heading to.
</Step>
</Steps>

## After you sign in

- **Privacy choices.** On a browser that has not been asked before, Hive3 shows **Your privacy choices**. Select <UI>Reject non-essential</UI>, <UI>Manage choices</UI> or <UI>Accept all</UI>. Necessary storage for signing in always stays on.
- **Terms.** If you need to agree to the current Terms and Conditions or Acceptable Use Policy, Hive3 asks separately after you sign in.
- **Email.** A banner may ask you to <UI>Verify email</UI>, or to <UI>Add email</UI> if you signed in without one. A verified email lets you always sign in with it. Select <UI>Not now</UI> to hide the banner for a while.
- **Suggested next steps.** Hive3 may suggest completing your profile, finding a community to join or browsing events. Each suggestion can be dismissed.

## Use more than one account

You can link up to 10 Hive3 accounts to the same browser and move between them. See [Find your way around](/start/find-your-way-around#switch-between-accounts).
