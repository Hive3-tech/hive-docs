Terms are listed alphabetically. Names of buttons and menu items appear exactly as they do in Hive3.

## A

**Account.** What you sign in as. Every account has a display name, a handle, a public account page and a plan. See [What is Hive3](/start/what-is-hive3#accounts-and-communities).

**Account Builder.** A card on your dashboard that lets you edit your public account page. See [Build your account page](/create/build-your-page).

**Add-ons.** Extras that communities publish and share in the Honey Marketplace. You create one from your dashboard. See [Create an add-on](/create/create-an-add-on).

**Admin.** A role that can manage an account's members, roles and settings. See [Roles and permissions](/community/roles-and-permissions).

**Affiliates.** Accounts connected to yours, such as sub-communities. Managed from the <UI>Affiliates</UI> card on your dashboard. Needs Honey Bee or higher. See [Affiliates](/community/affiliates).

**AI assistant.** A chat assistant a community can set up, name and train on its own documents. Needs Honey Bee or higher. See [AI assistant](/community/ai-assistant).

**API key.** A secret that lets a program act on your account through the Hive3 API or command-line tool. Needs Honey Bee or higher. See [Authentication and API keys](/developers/api-authentication).

**Application.** A request to join a private community. The button reads <UI>Apply to Join</UI>, and the community accepts or declines it. See [Members and applications](/community/members-and-applications).

## B

**Broadcasts.** Two things share this name. The <UI>Broadcasts</UI> card on a dashboard sends an email to a community's opted-in members, as often as the plan allows. The <UI>Broadcasts</UI> tab on a dashboard lists recordings of past live streams. See [Email broadcasts](/community/broadcasts) and [Go live](/create/go-live).

## C

**Camera Liveness Check.** An optional check in Settings that uses your camera and runs on your device. It is not an identity check. See [Liveness and proof of human](/account/proof-of-human).

**Community.** An account that is on a paid plan and is listed on the <UI>Communities</UI> page. See [What is Hive3](/start/what-is-hive3#accounts-and-communities).

**Credential.** A digital badge for finishing something, such as an event, course or quest, or awarded by a community. You claim it to a wallet. Creating your own credentials needs Honey Bee or higher. See [Your credentials](/account/credentials) and [Create and award credentials](/community/issue-credentials).

## D

**Dashboard.** Where you publish and manage. Open it with <UI>My Dashboard</UI>. See [Your dashboard](/create/your-dashboard).

**Discover.** The search page for communities, accounts, events, courses, quests and content. See [Discover and join communities](/explore/discover-and-join).

## H

**Handle.** Your unique name, shown with an @. It is how people find and mention you.

**Honey Bee.** The first paid plan, $75 a month. See [Plans and pricing](/start/plans).

**Honey Marketplace.** The directory of add-ons, in the sidebar. See [Create an add-on](/create/create-an-add-on).

## M

**Member.** Someone who has joined a community. See [Members and applications](/community/members-and-applications).

**Metaverse time.** Time you can book for events in 3D worlds. Swarm includes an hour a month, and others buy one-hour packs. See [Host in a 3D world](/create/host-in-a-world).

**Moderator.** A role that helps run a community. See [Roles and permissions](/community/roles-and-permissions).

**My Hive.** Your personal page listing everything you have joined or enrolled in. See [Find your way around](/start/find-your-way-around#my-hive).

## N

**Notifications.** Your list of updates and credentials you can claim, opened from the bell icon. See [Notifications](/explore/notifications).

## O

**Owner.** The person with full control of an account.

## P

**Plan.** The membership an account is on: Worker Bee, Honey Bee, Swarm or Royal Hive. See [Plans and pricing](/start/plans).

## Q

**Quest.** A set of objectives that people complete and submit evidence for, with a credential as a possible reward. See [Complete quests](/explore/complete-quests).

**Quest credit.** What you spend to publish a quest. A 7-day credit allows a quest up to 7 days long and a 30-day credit up to 30 days. See [Plans and pricing](/start/plans#quest-credits).

## R

**Referral code.** A short code you can share. People who sign up with it are counted as your referrals. See [Referrals](/account/referrals).

**Royal Hive.** The top plan, $550 a month, with its own dedicated 3D world. See [Plans and pricing](/start/plans).

## S

**Subscription.** Paid access to a community, its content, events or courses, with a price set by the community. Separate from your Hive3 plan. See [Tickets and subscriptions](/explore/tickets-and-subscriptions).

**Swarm.** The third plan, $250 a month, with monthly quest credits and metaverse time. See [Plans and pricing](/start/plans).

## T

**The Buzz.** The home feed of posts. See [Posts and the Buzz](/explore/the-buzz).

**Ticket.** A one-time payment for access to an event, course or quest. See [Tickets and subscriptions](/explore/tickets-and-subscriptions).

## W

**Wallet.** Where claimed credentials are held. You can create a Hive3 wallet, secured by a passkey on your device, or connect your own. See [Wallets](/account/wallets).

**Worker Bee.** The free plan. See [Plans and pricing](/start/plans).

**World.** A 3D space where events can happen. Shared worlds hold up to 500 people and a Royal Hive's dedicated world holds up to 100. See [Host in a 3D world](/create/host-in-a-world).
