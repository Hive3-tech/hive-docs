Hive3 has one main navigation, and it works the same way whether you are exploring or running a community. On a computer it is a sidebar on the left. On a phone it is a bar along the bottom with a menu that slides in from the left.

You can browse Discover, Events, Courses, Content, Quests, Jobs and the Honey Marketplace without signing in. Opening My Hive, your account, messages or notifications asks you to sign in or create an account first.

## The sidebar

The sidebar is split into four parts, from top to bottom.

<Figure src="/media/start/find-your-way-around/sidebar.webp" alt="The Hive3 desktop sidebar showing the account menu, four quick-action icons, the main navigation list and Settings" caption="The sidebar on a computer." />

### Your account menu

At the top you see your photo, name and handle. Select it to open a menu with:

- <UI>View Account</UI>: your public account page.
- <UI>My Dashboard</UI>: where you publish and manage. See [Your dashboard](/create/your-dashboard).
- <UI>Settings</UI>.
- <UI>Switch account</UI>.
- <UI>Log Out</UI>.

When you are signed out, this spot shows a <UI>Sign In</UI> button.

### Quick actions

Four icon buttons sit under your name:

| Icon | What it opens |
|---|---|
| Home | The home page with The Buzz feed. |
| Notifications | Your notifications. A number shows how many are unread. |
| Chats | Your conversations. See [Messages](/explore/messages). |
| Create post | A window for writing a post. |

### Main navigation

| Item | What you find there |
|---|---|
| <UI>Discover</UI> | Search across communities, accounts, events, courses, quests and content. |
| <UI>My Hive</UI> | Everything you have joined or enrolled in. |
| <UI>Events</UI> | The events directory. |
| <UI>Courses</UI> | The courses directory. |
| <UI>Content</UI> | Articles, videos and tutorials. |
| <UI>Quests</UI> | The quests directory. |
| <UI>Jobs</UI> | Open jobs. |
| <UI>Honey Marketplace</UI> | Add-ons and bundled services that communities publish. |
| <UI>Account</UI> | Your own account page. |

### Settings

<UI>Settings</UI> sits at the bottom. See the [Settings overview](/account/settings).

## Home and The Buzz

The home page opens with a welcome banner and <UI>Explore Communities</UI>, which takes you to the Communities page. Below it, **The Buzz** is the feed of posts from the communities and accounts you have joined. On a phone, <UI>Featured</UI> appears as a second tab next to The Buzz. See [Posts and the Buzz](/explore/the-buzz).

## Discover

<UI>Discover</UI> is the search page. Type into the <UI>Search for anything...</UI> field and narrow the results with the tabs <UI>All</UI>, <UI>Communities</UI>, <UI>Accounts</UI>, <UI>Events</UI>, <UI>Courses</UI>, <UI>Quests</UI> and <UI>Content</UI>. See [Discover and join communities](/explore/discover-and-join).

<Figure src="/media/start/find-your-way-around/discover.webp" alt="The Discover page with a search field and tabs for All, Communities, Accounts, Events, Courses, Quests and Content" caption="Discover." />

## My Hive

<UI>My Hive</UI> is your personal home base. Its description reads "Everything you're part of", and it has a tab for each kind of thing you are part of.

The tabs are <UI>Communities</UI>, <UI>Accounts</UI>, <UI>Events</UI>, <UI>Courses</UI>, <UI>Quests</UI> and <UI>Jobs</UI>. The <UI>My Dashboard</UI> button in the top corner opens your dashboard.

<Figure src="/media/start/find-your-way-around/my-hive.webp" alt="The My Hive page with tabs for Communities, Accounts, Events, Courses, Quests and Jobs and a My Dashboard button" caption="My Hive." />

## On a phone

A floating bar sits at the bottom of the screen with your photo and these icons:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Your photo | Opens the navigation menu: your account menu, Notifications, Events, Courses, Content, Quests, Jobs, Honey Marketplace, Account and Settings. |
| Home | Opens the home page. |
| Discover | Opens Discover. |
| My Hive | Opens My Hive. |
| Messages | Opens your conversations. |
| Plus (+) | Opens a window for writing a post. |

A number on your photo shows unread notifications. The bar hides while you are inside a conversation. Swipe left on the menu, or tap outside it, to close it.

<Figure src="/media/start/find-your-way-around/mobile-nav.webp" alt="A phone screen with the floating bottom bar: profile photo, Home, Discover, My Hive, Messages and a plus button" caption="The bottom bar on a phone." />

## Switch between accounts

You can link several Hive3 accounts and switch between them without signing out and in by hand.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the account menu">
Select your name at the top of the sidebar, then <UI>Switch account</UI>.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick an account">
The list shows your linked accounts. Select one to sign in as it. Your current account is marked <UI>Signed in</UI>.
</Step>
<Step title="Link another account (optional)">
Select <UI>Link account</UI>, then sign in to the account you want to add. You can switch back at any time from the same list. You can link up to 10 accounts.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Figure src="/media/start/find-your-way-around/switch-account.webp" alt="The Switch account window listing the signed-in account and linked accounts, with a Link account button" caption="Switch account." />

Select the X next to an account in the list to remove it from your accounts.

<Callout type="note">
Switching accounts signs you in to a different Hive3 login. To publish as a community you manage without switching, open that community's page and select the gear button labelled <UI>Dashboard</UI> in its header. See [What is Hive3](/start/what-is-hive3#acting-as-an-account-you-manage).
</Callout>
