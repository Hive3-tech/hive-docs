## Choose a plan

Worker Bee is free and lets you create events, courses, content, jobs, quests, add-ons and live streams. Honey Bee ($75 a month) adds private and paid access and community tools. Swarm ($250 a month) adds monthly quest credits and 3D world time. Royal Hive ($550 a month) gives you your own dedicated 3D world.

See the full comparison on [Plans and pricing](/start/plans).
