If your members already talk on Discord or Telegram, you do not have to choose between the two. A chat integration connects a Discord channel or a Telegram group to your community's chat, so messages can flow between them in the direction you choose.

**Who can do this:** the owner, admins and moderators of a community on [Honey Bee or higher](/start/plans). You also need to be an administrator of the Discord server or Telegram group you are connecting.

## Before you start

- Your community chat is the shared chat for your members. See [Messages](/explore/messages).
- Decide which channel (Discord) or group (Telegram) should be connected. Each integration links one target.

## Connect Discord

<Path items={["My Hive", "My Dashboard", "Chat Settings"]} />

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Chat Settings">
On your dashboard, select <UI>Chat Settings</UI>. The page is titled <UI>Chat Settings</UI> and starts with a short <UI>Instructions</UI> card.
</Step>
<Step title="Add the Hive3 bot to your server">
In the <UI>Discord Bot</UI> card, select <UI>Add Bot</UI>. Discord asks you to choose a server and approve the permissions the bot needs: view channels, send messages, manage messages, read message history and manage webhooks.
</Step>
<Step title="Add the integration">
Under <UI>Bot Integrations</UI>, select <UI>Add Integration</UI>. In the <UI>Add Bot Integration</UI> window, keep <UI>Platform</UI> set to <UI>Discord</UI>.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose the channel">
Enter your <UI>Discord Server ID</UI> and select <UI>Load channels</UI>. Then pick a text channel under <UI>Discord Channel</UI>.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose a sync direction">
Pick <UI>Bidirectional</UI>, <UI>Inbound</UI> or <UI>Outbound</UI>. See the table below. Select <UI>Add</UI>.
</Step>
<Step title="Verify">
The integration appears with a <UI>verification pending</UI> badge and a command that starts with `/hive-verify`. Post that exact command in the Discord channel as a server administrator. See [Verify the connection](#verify-the-connection).
</Step>
</Steps>

## Connect Telegram

<Steps>
<Step title="Add the bot to your group">
In the <UI>Telegram Bot</UI> card, select <UI>Add Bot</UI>, or add `@hive3_bot` to your Telegram group yourself. Make the bot an administrator of the group.
</Step>
<Step title="Add the integration">
Select <UI>Add Integration</UI>, set <UI>Platform</UI> to <UI>Telegram</UI>, and enter the <UI>Telegram Group ID</UI>.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose a sync direction and add it">
Pick a <UI>Sync Direction</UI> and select <UI>Add</UI>.
</Step>
<Step title="Verify">
Post the displayed `/hive-verify` command in the group as a group administrator.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Choose a sync direction

| Direction | What it does |
|---|---|
| <UI>Bidirectional</UI> | Messages go both ways: from Discord or Telegram into your community chat, and from your community chat out to Discord or Telegram. |
| <UI>Inbound</UI> | Messages from Discord or Telegram appear in your community chat. Nothing is sent back. |
| <UI>Outbound</UI> | Messages from your community chat are sent to Discord or Telegram. Nothing comes back. |

You can change the direction later from the integration's own menu.

## Verify the connection

An integration does nothing until Hive3 has confirmed that you control the Discord server or Telegram group. Until then its badge reads <UI>verification pending</UI> and the controls are locked.

1. Copy the `/hive-verify` command shown on the integration.
2. Post it, exactly as shown, in the connected Discord channel or Telegram group. You must be an administrator there.
3. Hive3 recognizes the command and the badge changes to <UI>active</UI>.

The command expires after 7 days. If it does, delete the integration and add it again to get a fresh one.

## Manage an integration

Each integration in the list shows its platform, the channel or group ID, and a status badge.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Direction menu | Change between Bidirectional, Inbound and Outbound. |
| <UI>Pause</UI> or <UI>Resume</UI> | Stop or restart the relay without deleting it. |
| <UI>Delete</UI> | Remove the integration. |

The badge tells you the state: <UI>active</UI>, <UI>paused</UI> or <UI>verification pending</UI>. A <UI>delivery issue</UI> badge means Hive3 had trouble delivering a message, and hovering over it shows the reason. After repeated delivery failures an integration is switched off and marked <UI>auto-paused</UI>. Fix the cause, such as a removed bot or changed permissions, then select <UI>Resume</UI>.

## Permissions to check

- **Discord, deleting messages.** To delete a Discord-written message from the Hive3 side, the bot needs the Manage Messages permission. If you connected before this was required, invite the bot again with <UI>Add Bot</UI> or grant that permission in Discord.
- **Telegram, reactions.** Reactions only sync when the bot is a group administrator.
