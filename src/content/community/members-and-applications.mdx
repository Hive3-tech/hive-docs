A member is anyone who has joined your community. You decide how people get in: instantly, after you approve them, or after they subscribe. The Members page is where you review applications and look after the people who have joined.

**Who can do this:** the owner, admins and moderators can review applications and open the Members page. Requiring approval or a subscription needs a [Honey Bee or higher](/start/plans) plan. See [Roles and permissions](/community/roles-and-permissions) for what each role can do.

## Choose how people join

Your community's access setting controls what visitors see on your page.

<Path items={["My Hive", "My Dashboard", "Account Builder", "Community settings"]} />

Open your dashboard (or the dashboard of the community you manage), choose <UI>Account Builder</UI>, then open <UI>Community settings</UI>. Under <UI>Community access</UI> you pick one of four options:

| Option | What it means | What visitors see |
|---|---|---|
| <UI>Open</UI> | Anyone can follow instantly. | <UI>Join Community</UI> |
| <UI>Private</UI> | Require approval before someone can follow. | <UI>Apply to Join</UI>, then <UI>Pending Approval</UI> until you decide |
| <UI>Open + Subscription</UI> | A paid subscription is required to follow. | <UI>Subscribe</UI> |
| <UI>Private + Subscription</UI> | Require both approval and a paid subscription. | <UI>Apply to Join</UI>, then <UI>Subscribe</UI> once approved |

<Callout type="plan">
Worker Bee accounts can only use <UI>Open</UI>. The other three options come with Honey Bee.
</Callout>

The two subscription options stay unavailable until you have created at least one subscription plan. When you choose one, a <UI>Required subscription plan</UI> menu appears so you can pick which plan unlocks your community. See [Get paid](/community/get-paid) to create a plan.

## Open the Members page

<Path items={["My Hive", "My Dashboard", "Members"]} />

The <UI>Members</UI> card on your dashboard shows how many members you have. When applications are waiting, it also shows how many are pending, with a red badge on the icon.

The page is titled <UI>Account Members</UI>. Across the top you see the totals for <UI>Total Members</UI>, <UI>Active Members</UI> and <UI>Staff Members</UI> (owners, admins and moderators together).

## Review applications

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the pending list">
Select <UI>Pending</UI>. The number in brackets is how many applications are waiting.
</Step>
<Step title="Read each application">
Every card shows the applicant's name and email address, and when they applied.
</Step>
<Step title="Decide">
Select <UI>Approve</UI> to let the person in, or <UI>Deny</UI> to turn the application down. An approved applicant becomes a member straight away.
</Step>
</Steps>

When someone applies, Hive3 sends a notification to the owner and to every admin. Moderators can review applications from the Members page but are not sent the notification.

<Callout type="tip">
Select <UI>Pending</UI> again to return to the full member list.
</Callout>

## Find people in your member list

- Type in the search box to find a member by name or handle.
- Choose <UI>Filter by Role</UI> and pick <UI>All Roles</UI>, <UI>Owner</UI>, <UI>Admin</UI>, <UI>Moderator</UI> or <UI>Member</UI>.

The table shows each member's role, when they joined, their subscription and their status.

## Message or remove a member

Open the three-dot menu at the end of a member's row.

| Menu item | What it does | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| <UI>Send Message</UI> | Opens a new direct message to that person. | Everyone who can open the page |
| <UI>Change Role</UI> | Opens [Permissions](/community/roles-and-permissions). | Owner and admins |
| <UI>Remove Member</UI> | Removes the person from your community. | See below |

### Remove a member

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose Remove Member">
Open the member's menu and select <UI>Remove Member</UI>.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm">
Type `remove this member` in the box, then select <UI>Remove member</UI>. This ends their membership and any role they held.
</Step>
</Steps>

A few rules apply:

- Moderators can remove regular members only. Owners and admins can also remove admins and moderators.
- The owner can never be removed.
- You cannot remove someone who still has paid access. The dialog tells you the date their current billing period ends. Wait until it passes so they receive the full term they paid for.

## What happens next

- Members can see your members-only content once they are in.
- To change who can help you manage applications, see [Roles and permissions](/community/roles-and-permissions).
- To send everyone an update, see [Email broadcasts](/community/broadcasts).
