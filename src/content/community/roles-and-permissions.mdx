You do not have to run a community alone. Roles let you hand out parts of the work: an admin can manage almost everything, and a moderator can look after people and day-to-day content without touching money or roles.

**Who can do this:** the owner and admins can assign roles. Adding admins or moderators comes with [Honey Bee and above](/start/plans).

## The three roles

Everyone who joins starts as a regular member. On top of that, an account has three levels of staff.

- **Owner.** The account itself. The owner has every permission, can never be removed, cannot leave, and cannot have the role changed.
- **Admin.** Everything the owner can do for the community, including managing roles and payments, except changing or removing the owner.
- **Moderator.** Runs the community day to day: members, content, broadcasts and events. A moderator cannot manage roles or payments.

## What each role can do

| Task | Owner | Admin | Moderator |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Approve or deny applications | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Receive a notification when someone applies | Yes | Yes | No |
| Remove regular members | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Remove admins and moderators | Yes | Yes | No |
| Assign, change and remove roles in <UI>Permissions</UI> | Yes | Yes | No |
| Edit the account page, welcome page and community access | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Publish and edit events, courses, content, jobs, quests and add-ons | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Review enrollments, applications and quest evidence | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Delete posts and comments in the community | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Send [email broadcasts](/community/broadcasts) | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Set up the [AI assistant](/community/ai-assistant) | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Connect [Discord and Telegram](/community/chat-integrations) | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Manage [affiliates](/community/affiliates) | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| [Create and award credentials](/community/issue-credentials) | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Go live, and book and manage worlds | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Connect Stripe or PayPal, manage subscriptions and tickets, approve refunds in <UI>Monetize</UI> | Yes | Yes | No |
| Buy quest credits and metaverse time | Yes | Yes | No |

A moderator who creates a quest uses the community's existing quest credits but cannot buy more.

Your own **Account Settings**, **Wallets** and **API Keys** cards appear on your own dashboard only. They belong to you personally and are not shared with the people you give roles to.

<Callout type="note">
The <UI>Permissions</UI> and <UI>Monetize</UI> cards are hidden from moderators on the dashboard, and the Permissions page redirects them back to the account page.
</Callout>

## Assign a role

<Path items={["My Hive", "My Dashboard", "Permissions"]} />

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Permissions">
On your dashboard, select <UI>Permissions</UI>. The page lists your owners, admins and moderators, with a count for each.
</Step>
<Step title="Start a new permission">
Select <UI>New Permission</UI>. The <UI>Assign New Role</UI> window opens.
</Step>
<Step title="Find the person">
Type a name or handle into <UI>Search by name or @handle...</UI> and choose the right person from the results. Select <UI>Clear</UI> if you picked the wrong one.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose a role">
Pick <UI>Moderator</UI> or <UI>Admin</UI>.
</Step>
<Step title="Assign">
Select <UI>Assign Role</UI>. The role takes effect right away, and the person can open your dashboard from <UI>Accounts you manage</UI>.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Change or remove a role

On the <UI>Permissions</UI> page, find the person under <UI>Admins</UI> or <UI>Moderators</UI>.

- Use the role menu beside their name to switch between <UI>Admin</UI> and <UI>Moderator</UI>.
- Select <UI>Remove</UI> to take the role away. They stay in your community as a regular member.

You can also reach this page from the Members page: open a member's menu and choose <UI>Change Role</UI>.

## Work on a community you help manage

If someone gave you a role, you do not need to be the owner to reach the dashboard.

<Path items={["My Hive", "My Dashboard", "Accounts you manage"]} />

<UI>Accounts you manage</UI> lists every account where you are an admin or a moderator. Open one to reach its dashboard. What you see depends on your role: moderators do not see <UI>Permissions</UI> or <UI>Monetize</UI>.

## Good habits

- Start people as moderators and promote them to admin only when they need to manage roles or payments.
- Admins can assign and remove other admins, so give the role only to people you fully trust.
- Review the <UI>Permissions</UI> page after a team change.
