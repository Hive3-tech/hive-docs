Affiliates are the communities and organizations you want to be seen alongside: partners, sponsors, chapters or sub-communities. They appear together on an <UI>Affiliates</UI> tab on your community page, so visitors can find them from you.

**Who can do this:** the owner, admins and moderators of a community on [Honey Bee or higher](/start/plans).

## What affiliates are, and are not

An affiliate is a **connection that is displayed**. It is a way to say "we work with them".

- It does show the affiliate on your page, in the groups you choose.
- It does not pay anyone. There are no commissions, revenue shares or payouts.
- It does not share members, roles, content or permissions between the two communities.
- It is not the referral program. To invite people and track signups, see [Referrals](/account/referrals).

## Open the Affiliates page

<Path items={["My Hive", "My Dashboard", "Affiliates"]} />

The page is titled <UI>Affiliates</UI>. When you have none yet, it says <UI>No affiliates yet</UI> and invites you to add an affiliate or sub-account.

## Add an affiliate

You can connect to another community on Hive3, or list an organization that is not on Hive3.

### Connect to a community on Hive3

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the form">
Select <UI>Add affiliate</UI>.
</Step>
<Step title="Find the community">
Type in <UI>Search Hive</UI> and choose the account from the results.
</Step>
<Step title="Add a message (optional)">
Write some context for the other team under <UI>Message (optional)</UI>.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick groups (optional)">
Under <UI>Add to groups (optional)</UI>, choose the groups the affiliate should appear in.
</Step>
<Step title="Send">
Select <UI>Send request</UI>. The other community has to accept before the connection appears on your page.
</Step>
</Steps>

Your request shows under <UI>Sent requests</UI>, where you can select <UI>Cancel</UI> until it is answered. If the other community declines, you have to wait 24 hours before you can send another request to it.

### List an organization that is not on Hive3

Select <UI>Not on Hive? Add manually</UI>, then fill in the <UI>Name</UI>, <UI>Website URL</UI> and <UI>About</UI>, and optionally upload a <UI>Logo image</UI> and a <UI>Banner image</UI>. Select <UI>Add affiliate</UI>. An outside organization is added straight away because there is nobody on Hive3 to approve it. You can change its details later with <UI>Edit</UI> from its menu.

## Answer a request

When another community asks to connect with you, the request appears under <UI>Pending requests</UI>. Select <UI>Accept</UI> or <UI>Decline</UI>. Accepted connections show under <UI>Affiliated with</UI>.

## Organize affiliates into groups

Groups keep a long list tidy, for example <UI>Partners</UI>, <UI>Sponsors</UI> or <UI>Chapters</UI>. On your community page, each group becomes a tab.

<Steps>
<Step title="Create a group">
Select <UI>New group</UI>, type a <UI>Group name</UI> (up to 60 characters) and select <UI>Create group</UI>. You can have up to 50 groups.
</Step>
<Step title="Put affiliates in it">
Open the group's tab and select <UI>Choose affiliates</UI>, or open an affiliate's menu and choose <UI>Edit groups</UI>.
</Step>
<Step title="Rename or delete">
Use the group options menu for <UI>Rename group</UI> or <UI>Delete group</UI>. Deleting a group removes only the group and its tab. Its affiliates stay on your page under <UI>All</UI>.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Remove an affiliate

Open an affiliate's menu and select <UI>Delete</UI>. This removes the connection, including from any groups, and cannot be undone. Either community in a connection can remove it.
