A credential is a digital badge that someone can claim and keep in their wallet. Use one to recognize a contributor, mark a milestone, or give members something to show for being part of your community.

**Who can do this:** the owner, admins and moderators of a community on [Honey Bee or higher](/start/plans).

<Callout type="note">
Hive3 already sets up a credential for you when you publish an event, course or quest on a paid account. This page is about **custom credentials**: badges you create yourself and award to the people you choose, or let anyone claim with a link.
</Callout>

## Choose the network first

New credentials are set up automatically on the network you choose in Settings. Existing credentials keep the network they were created on.

<Path items={["Settings", "Credentials"]} />

Under <UI>Credential network</UI>, pick a network from the cards. A card marked <UI>Claimers use their own wallet</UI> is a network where Hive3 wallets are not available, so people claiming on it must bring their own wallet. See [Wallets](/account/wallets).

## Create a credential

The credential wizard has three steps: **Metadata**, **Configuration** and **Review**. Open it at `app.hive3.tech/create/credential`. If you are not on a paid plan, the page tells you credentials are included with Honey Bee and above, with an <UI>Upgrade</UI> button.

### Step 1: Metadata

<Steps>
<Step title="Name it">
Enter a <UI>Credential Name</UI> of up to 100 characters, for example Event Attendee Badge. It is required.
</Step>
<Step title="Describe it">
Write a <UI>Description</UI> of up to 1,000 characters that says what the credential represents. It is required.
</Step>
<Step title="Add an image">
Hive3 generates a badge from the name. To use your own artwork instead, select <UI>Upload image</UI> and choose a PNG, JPG, GIF or WebP image between 512 and 4096 pixels on each side and up to 10 MB. Once you have uploaded your own, the reset button on the image (<UI>Use generated badge</UI>) brings the generated badge back.
</Step>
<Step title="Add attributes (optional)">
Select <UI>Add Attribute</UI> to add details such as a rarity or category. Each attribute has a trait type and a value.
</Step>
<Step title="Continue">
Select <UI>Continue</UI>.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Step 2: Configuration

<Steps>
<Step title="Set the supply">
Under <UI>Maximum Supply</UI>, enter the most credentials that can be claimed. Leave it at 0 for unlimited supply.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose who can claim">
Under <UI>Who can claim</UI>, pick one:
- <UI>Anyone with the link</UI>: anyone who opens the credential's page can claim it.
- <UI>Only people I award it to</UI>: only people you award it to can claim it.
</Step>
<Step title="Continue">
Check the <UI>Configuration Summary</UI> and select <UI>Continue</UI>.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Step 3: Review

The <UI>Review & Create</UI> step shows your credential summary: name, description, image, attributes, supply and who can claim. Select <UI>Previous</UI> to change anything, or <UI>Create Credential</UI> to finish.

<Figure
  src="/media/community/issue-credentials/review-step.webp"
  alt="The Review and Create step of the credential wizard showing the credential summary, supply and claim access."
  caption="The Review step."
/>

You land on the credential's own page. Hive3 sets the credential up on the network in the background, and the page shows its progress, such as <UI>Setting up on</UI> followed by the network name. If setup fails, Hive3 retries automatically, and you can select <UI>Retry</UI> to try again yourself. People can claim once setup is confirmed.

<Callout type="tip">
Bookmark the credential's page. It is where you award it, and where you copy the link to share when anyone can claim.
</Callout>

## Award a credential to people

Awarding picks the people who can claim. It is available for custom credentials, whether they are open to anyone or award-only.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the credential">
Go to the credential's page. As a manager you see an <UI>Award to people</UI> button below the details.
</Step>
<Step title="Enter handles">
Select <UI>Award to people</UI>. In the <UI>Award credential</UI> window, type the handles of the people you want to reward, such as `@alice, @bob`. Separate up to 50 handles with commas, spaces or new lines.
</Step>
<Step title="Award">
Select <UI>Award credential</UI>. A message confirms how many people were awarded and lists any handles Hive3 could not find.
</Step>
</Steps>

Each person you award gets a notification that reads "You've been awarded a credential" and asks them to claim it. Awarding someone who already has the award does nothing, so it is safe to paste the same list twice.

## What people do next

Recipients open the notification, select the credential, and claim it with a wallet. They can use their Hive3 wallet, secured by a passkey on their device, or a wallet they already own. The steps for them are in [Your credentials](/account/credentials).
