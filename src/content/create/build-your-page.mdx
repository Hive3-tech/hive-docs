Your account page is what people see when they find you on Hive3. The Account Builder lets you edit the page directly: change the banner and name, choose which sections appear, hand-pick what each section shows, and decide who can join.

**Who can do this:** the owner, admins and moderators of the account. Every plan can build a page. The <UI>Private</UI> and subscription access options need a [Honey Bee or higher](/start/plans) plan.

## Open the Account Builder

<Path items={["My Hive", "My Dashboard", "Account Builder"]} />

Open the dashboard of the account you want to edit, then select the <UI>Account Builder</UI> card. If you manage a community, open [its dashboard](/create/your-dashboard) first.

The builder replaces the usual sidebar with a controls panel headed <UI>Community Builder</UI>. Your page sits beside it as a live preview. The panel has three areas under <UI>Controls menu</UI>:

| Area | What it does |
|---|---|
| <UI>Community settings</UI> | Who can join. See [Members and applications](/community/members-and-applications). |
| <UI>Manage widget</UI> | The widget column on the right of your page. |
| <UI>Manage overview</UI> | The sections on your page. |

<Figure
  src="/media/create/build-your-page/builder.webp"
  alt="The Account Builder with the Controls menu on the left and the account page preview on the right."
  caption="The Account Builder."
/>

Nothing you change is public until you save. The panel shows <UI>You have unsaved Community Builder changes.</UI> while edits are waiting. Select <UI>Save and exit</UI> to publish them, or <UI>Cancel</UI> to discard them. If you try to leave with unsaved changes, Hive3 asks whether to keep editing, discard or save.

## Edit your banner, name and details

The top of the preview is editable. Hover over a part of it and select it to change it.

<Steps>
<Step title="Change the banner and community image">
Select the banner to upload a wide cover image, or the community image to upload your logo. Each can be removed again.
</Step>
<Step title="Change the name and short description">
Select the name to edit it. Your <UI>Community name</UI> can be up to 80 characters and the <UI>Short description</UI> up to 100.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose a handle">
Select <UI>Edit handle</UI>. A handle is 3 to 30 characters: lowercase letters, numbers, underscores and hyphens, starting with a letter or number. Hive3 checks that it is available before you can save.
</Step>
<Step title="Add a location, badges and links">
Add a <UI>Location</UI> (up to 80 characters), up to 5 badges of up to 24 characters each, and your social links.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Choose the sections on your page

Open <UI>Manage overview</UI>. Sections that are switched on appear first under <UI>Active</UI>, with a count. Switched-off sections are grouped below by category, each with how many are <UI>available</UI>.

| Category | Sections |
|---|---|
| <UI>Featured</UI> | <UI>Spotlight</UI>, one highlighted item or a welcome message |
| <UI>Story</UI> | <UI>About</UI>, <UI>Guidelines</UI> |
| <UI>Showcase</UI> | <UI>Subscriptions</UI>, <UI>Events</UI>, <UI>Courses</UI>, <UI>Quests</UI>, <UI>Jobs</UI> |
| <UI>Credentials</UI> | <UI>Credentials</UI>, the credentials your community has issued |

<Steps>
<Step title="Switch a section on or off">
Use the toggle on its row.
</Step>
<Step title="Reorder the active sections">
Drag a row, or use its up and down arrows.
</Step>
<Step title="Edit a section">
Select <UI>Edit</UI> on its row. What opens depends on the section, as described below.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Figure
  src="/media/create/build-your-page/manage-overview.webp"
  alt="The Manage Overview panel with the Active list at the top and the Featured, Story, Showcase and Credentials categories below."
  caption="Manage overview."
/>

### Spotlight

Spotlight is the first thing visitors see. In <UI>Edit spotlight</UI>, pick what to feature: <UI>Off</UI>, <UI>Welcome</UI> (your own message), <UI>Subscription</UI>, <UI>Event</UI>, <UI>Course</UI>, <UI>Quest</UI> or <UI>Job</UI>. For an item, choose which one. You can also set a <UI>Headline</UI>, a <UI>Description</UI>, a banner image (<UI>Upload banner</UI>) and a <UI>Button label</UI>. For a welcome message, these fields are the message itself.

### About and Guidelines

<UI>About</UI> opens a rich text editor for your long introduction, up to 3,000 words. <UI>Guidelines</UI> lets you add numbered rules, each with a title and a description, using <UI>Add guideline</UI>. <UI>About</UI> only appears on your page once it has content.

### Events, courses, quests, jobs, subscriptions and credentials

These sections open a window with the same options:

- <UI>Section title</UI> renames the heading.
- <UI>Display mode</UI> is either <UI>All current items</UI>, which always shows everything you have published, or <UI>Hand-picked</UI>, where you search, choose and order exactly which items appear under <UI>Selected order</UI>.
- A layout choice sets how many appear, from <UI>1 row</UI> up to <UI>All</UI>.

## Set who can join

Open <UI>Community settings</UI> to choose <UI>Open</UI>, <UI>Private</UI>, <UI>Open + Subscription</UI> or <UI>Private + Subscription</UI>. The full explanation is in [Members and applications](/community/members-and-applications).

## The right-hand widget column

Your page can show a separate column of widgets beside the main sections. In <UI>Manage widget</UI>, turn <UI>Right widget column</UI> on or off. When it is on, select <UI>Customize sidebar</UI> to switch widgets on or off and drag the enabled ones into order. <UI>Reset to default</UI> restores the starting layout.

## Your welcome page

There is no separate welcome page to maintain. People who are not signed in, and signed-in people who have not joined yet, see your page's sections under a welcome banner with the join button that matches your access setting (for example <UI>Join Account</UI>, <UI>Apply to Join</UI> or <UI>Sign In to Subscribe</UI> for visitors who are signed out). Whatever you switch on in the Account Builder is what they see, so your <UI>Spotlight</UI> works as your welcome message.

<Callout type="tip">
Check the result without the builder open: sign out, or open your page in a private window, and look at it the way a new visitor does.
</Callout>

If a page still has settings from an older welcome page, the builder imports them as a starting point and shows a note. Save once to move them across.

## Save your changes

Select <UI>Save and exit</UI> in the controls panel. Hive3 confirms with <UI>Community updated</UI> and returns you to your page. On a phone, open the menu, then use <UI>Visualize community</UI> to look at the preview and <UI>Cancel</UI> to leave without saving.

<Callout type="note">
<UI>Save and exit</UI> is unavailable while a field has an error, for example a handle that is already taken.
</Callout>
