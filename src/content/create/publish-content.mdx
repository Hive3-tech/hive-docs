Content is how you publish something to read: an article, a guide, a report or a PDF. You write it in Hive3's editor or upload a document, add a banner and a short summary, and decide who can read it. The form has two steps.

**Who can do this:** any account on any plan, and the owner, admins and moderators of a community. Members-only and subscription content need a [Honey Bee or higher](/start/plans) plan. Content from a Worker Bee account is always public.

## Start new content

<Path items={["My Hive", "My Dashboard", "Content", "New Content"]} />

Open the dashboard of the account that should publish, then select <UI>New Content</UI> on the <UI>Content</UI> tab. For a community, open [its dashboard](/create/your-dashboard) first.

The form is titled <UI>Create New Content</UI>. A panel on the right shows your two steps, a preview card, <UI>Back</UI> and <UI>Next</UI> buttons and a <UI>Drafts</UI> menu. Your progress is saved automatically as a draft, and <UI>Cancel</UI> offers <UI>Discard draft</UI>, <UI>Keep editing</UI> and <UI>Save & exit</UI>.

## Basic Info

<Steps>

Required. Up to 60 characters.

<Step title="Write the Short Description">
Required. Up to 125 characters, one or two sentences. It appears on content cards.

<Step title="Add the Full Description">
Required. Either write the content in the editor, or upload a document. See below.

Up to 5 badges from the suggestions, so people can find the content by topic.

### Write in the editor

The editor takes up to 3,000 words. Its toolbar covers:

| Tool | Options |
|---|---|
| Text | Paragraph, <UI>Heading 1</UI> to <UI>Heading 3</UI>, <UI>Bold</UI>, <UI>Italic</UI>, <UI>Underline</UI>, <UI>Strikethrough</UI>, <UI>Highlight</UI>, <UI>Inline code</UI>, subscript and superscript |
| Structure | <UI>Bullet list</UI>, <UI>Numbered list</UI>, <UI>Task list</UI>, <UI>Blockquote</UI>, <UI>Horizontal rule</UI>, <UI>Insert table</UI> |
| Layout | Align left, center, right and justify |
| Links and media | <UI>Add link</UI>, <UI>Upload image</UI>, <UI>Embed YouTube video</UI> |
| History | <UI>Undo</UI> and <UI>Redo</UI> |

Hive3 does not accept images pasted as raw data. Use <UI>Upload image</UI> so the file is stored with your content.

### Upload a document instead

Select <UI>Upload Document</UI> above the editor and choose a PDF or a Markdown file (`.pdf`, `.md` or `.markdown`). While it converts the button reads <UI>Converting...</UI>.

- A Markdown file is converted to formatted text.
- A PDF is shown to readers in a PDF viewer on the content page.

The uploaded file appears in place of the editor with a <UI>Remove</UI> button. If you have already written text, Hive3 asks <UI>This will replace the existing description. Continue?</UI> before it swaps it out. Select <UI>Remove</UI> to go back to the editor.

<Callout type="tip">
Upload a PDF when layout matters, such as a report or a slide deck. Use the editor when you want text that reads well on a phone.
</Callout>

## Content Access

This step is titled <UI>Content Access</UI>. It controls who can discover and read the content.

| Option | Who can read it |
|---|---|
| <UI>Public</UI> | Anyone can view this content. |
| <UI>Members only</UI> | Only members or collaborators can view this content. |
| <UI>Subscription</UI> | An active subscription is required before viewing. |

For <UI>Subscription</UI>, connect Stripe in <UI>Monetize</UI>, then choose a plan under <UI>Select subscription</UI>. If you have no plan yet, <UI>Create subscription</UI> takes you to your payment settings. See [Get paid](/community/get-paid). Worker Bee accounts see only <UI>Public</UI>.

## Publish

Select <UI>Create Content</UI> in the panel. Hive3 asks <UI>Ready to publish?</UI> and explains that it will create your content and take you to the published page. Choose <UI>Create Content</UI> to confirm, or <UI>Keep editing</UI> to go back. Hive3 then confirms with <UI>Content created successfully!</UI>

## After you publish

Your content appears on the <UI>Content</UI> tab of your [dashboard](/create/your-dashboard).

- <UI>Edit</UI> reopens the form as <UI>Edit Content</UI>. The last button reads <UI>Update Content</UI> and the confirmation reads <UI>Ready to update?</UI>.
- <UI>View</UI> opens the public page.
- <UI>More actions</UI> offers <UI>Hide from profile</UI>, which removes the content from your public lists, Discover and search without deleting it, and a <UI>Notifications on</UI> or <UI>Notifications off</UI> switch.
- <UI>Delete</UI> removes it after you confirm.

You can hide content at any time. There is no waiting period, as there is for events and quests.

## Tips

- Put the most useful sentence in the short description. It is all most people see before they open the page.
- Break long articles up with headings, so readers can scan the page.
- Use <UI>Members only</UI> for material you want your community to have first, and <UI>Subscription</UI> for material you want to charge for.
- Link to your related events, courses and quests inside the article to send readers onward.
