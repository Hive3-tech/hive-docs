A job posting tells the Hive3 community about a role you are hiring for. Applicants send a résumé and an optional cover letter through Hive3, and you review every application in one place. The form has two steps.

**Who can do this:** any account on any plan, and the owner, admins and moderators of a community. Limiting applications to members or subscribers needs a [Honey Bee or higher](/start/plans) plan. A Worker Bee posting is always public.

## Start a new job posting

<Path items={["My Hive", "My Dashboard", "Jobs", "New Job"]} />

Open the dashboard of the account that is hiring and select <UI>New Job</UI> on the <UI>Jobs</UI> tab. For a community, open [its dashboard](/create/your-dashboard) first so the posting appears under the community.

The form is titled <UI>Create Job Posting</UI>. A panel on the right shows your two steps, a preview card, <UI>Back</UI> and <UI>Next</UI> buttons and a <UI>Drafts</UI> menu. Hive3 saves your progress as a draft. <UI>Cancel</UI> offers <UI>Discard draft</UI>, <UI>Keep editing</UI> and <UI>Save & exit</UI>.

## Basic Info

<Steps>
<Step title="Add a Job Banner">
Optional. Upload a wide image; 1600 × 400 pixels is recommended. It is a good place for your logo or brand.
</Step>
<Step title="Enter the Job Title">
Required, for example "Senior Blockchain Developer".
</Step>
<Step title="Set the Job Location">
Enter a city, state or country. Leave it blank for a remote role; the posting then shows Remote.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose the Job Type">
Required. Select <UI>Full-time</UI>, <UI>Part-time</UI>, <UI>Contract</UI>, <UI>Freelance</UI> or <UI>Internship</UI>.
</Step>
<Step title="Add a Pay Range, if you want to">
Optional. Choose a currency (<UI>USD</UI>, <UI>EUR</UI>, <UI>GBP</UI> or <UI>ETH</UI>) and enter a <UI>Min</UI> and a <UI>Max</UI>.
</Step>
<Step title="Write the Job Description">
Required. Describe the responsibilities and requirements in the editor, up to 3,000 words, or select <UI>Upload Document</UI> and choose a PDF or Markdown file.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose badges">
Up to 5 badges from the suggestions, such as skills or fields.
</Step>
</Steps>

You cannot move on until the title, the job type and a description or document are in place.

## Job Access

This step is titled <UI>Job Access</UI>. Choose who can discover and apply.

| Option | Who can apply |
|---|---|
| <UI>Public</UI> | Anyone can view and apply. |
| <UI>Members only</UI> | Only members or collaborators can view and apply. |
| <UI>Subscription</UI> | An active subscription is required before applying. |

For <UI>Subscription</UI>, connect Stripe in <UI>Monetize</UI>, then pick a plan under <UI>Select subscription</UI>. If you have none yet, <UI>Create subscription</UI> takes you to your payment settings. See [Get paid](/community/get-paid).

## Publish

Select <UI>Create Job</UI>. Hive3 asks <UI>Ready to publish?</UI>. Choose <UI>Create Job</UI> to publish, and you land on the job page. When you edit a posting later, the buttons read <UI>Update Job</UI> and <UI>Ready to update?</UI>.

## Review applications

From the <UI>Jobs</UI> tab of your [dashboard](/create/your-dashboard), open <UI>More actions</UI> on the posting and select <UI>Manage Applications</UI>.

The page shows <UI>Total Applications</UI>, <UI>Pending Review</UI>, <UI>Approved</UI> and <UI>Rejected</UI> counts, and tabs to filter the list: <UI>All</UI>, <UI>Pending</UI>, <UI>Approved</UI> and <UI>Rejected</UI>.

<Steps>
<Step title="Read the application">
Select the <UI>View</UI> button for the résumé, which includes the file name, to open it. If the applicant wrote a cover letter, a second <UI>View</UI> button opens that.
</Step>
<Step title="Decide">
Select <UI>Approve</UI> or <UI>Reject</UI>.
</Step>
</Steps>

### What applicants see

An applicant selects <UI>Apply for Job</UI> and fills in a short form: an optional <UI>Cover Letter</UI> and a required <UI>Resume</UI>. If an application is rejected, the applicant can apply again, and the form is then titled <UI>Reapply for Job</UI>.

### Edit, hide or delete

<UI>Edit</UI> reopens the form as <UI>Edit Job Posting</UI>. <UI>Hide from profile</UI> in the <UI>More actions</UI> menu removes the posting from your public lists and from search without deleting it, and <UI>Delete</UI> removes it.

## Tips

- Say what the role is in the first sentence of the description. Applicants skim.
- Leave the pay range in when you can. Roles with a stated range are easier to evaluate.
- Use <UI>Members only</UI> to hire from your own community first.
- Ask applicants to attach everything you need to the résumé, since the application has a single résumé upload.
