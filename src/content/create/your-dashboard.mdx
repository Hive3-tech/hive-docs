Your dashboard is where you create and manage events, courses, content, jobs, quests and add-ons, check your quest credits and metaverse time, and reach the tools that run your community. Every guide in this section starts here.

**Who can do this:** every account has a dashboard. For a community, the owner, admins and moderators can open it. Owners and admins also see the money and role tools. Some cards need a [Honey Bee or higher](/start/plans) plan, as noted below.

## Open your dashboard

<Path items={["My Hive", "My Dashboard"]} />

Open <UI>My Hive</UI> in the sidebar and select <UI>My Dashboard</UI> at the top of the page. You can also open your account menu at the top of the sidebar and choose <UI>My Dashboard</UI>.

The page is titled <UI>Account Dashboard</UI>. It has three parts: a row of cards for tools and settings, a set of tabs for the things you have published, and two summary panels for metaverse time and quest credits.

## Switch to a community you manage

You have one dashboard for your own account and one for every community where you are an owner, admin or moderator. Anything you create from a dashboard is published as the account that dashboard belongs to, so open the right one before you start.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Accounts you manage">
On your own dashboard, select the <UI>Accounts you manage</UI> card. The page lists every account where you are an owner, admin or moderator, with your role on each card. If you do not manage any other account, it says <UI>You do not manage another account.</UI>
</Step>
<Step title="Open the community">
Select the community's card to open its public page.
</Step>
<Step title="Open its dashboard">
Select the gear button in the top-right corner of the community's banner. Its label is <UI>Dashboard</UI>. The button only appears for owners, admins and moderators.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Figure
  src="/media/create/your-dashboard/community-dashboard-button.webp"
  alt="The top-right corner of a community banner showing the Wallet, Share and gear Dashboard buttons."
  caption="The gear button on a community's page opens its dashboard."
/>

<Callout type="note">
<UI>Switch account</UI> in your account menu is a different feature. It signs you in as another login you have linked to Hive3. See [Find your way around](/start/find-your-way-around). Managing a community does not need a switch: you stay signed in as yourself.
</Callout>

## Manage what you have published

The tabs sit under the heading <UI>Manage Events</UI> (the heading follows the selected tab). Each tab shows how many items it holds.

| Tab | What it lists | Button to add one |
|---|---|---|
| <UI>Events</UI> | Events you have published, newest first | <UI>New Event</UI> |
| <UI>Courses</UI> | Courses, marked Draft or Published | <UI>New Course</UI> |
| <UI>Content</UI> | Articles and documents | <UI>New Content</UI> |
| <UI>Jobs</UI> | Job postings, marked Open or Closed | <UI>New Job</UI> |
| <UI>Quests</UI> | Quests, marked Scheduled, Active or Ended | <UI>New Quest</UI> |
| <UI>Broadcasts</UI> | Recordings of your past live streams | None. See [Go live](/create/go-live). |

<Callout type="note">
The <UI>Broadcasts</UI> tab holds recordings of live streams. The <UI>Broadcasts</UI> card in the row above is a different tool: it sends email updates to your members. See [Email broadcasts](/community/broadcasts).
</Callout>

Each item shows its banner, title, when you created it and badges such as <UI>IRL</UI> or <UI>Metaverse</UI>, <UI>Upcoming</UI>, <UI>Ongoing</UI> or <UI>Past</UI>, and <UI>Hidden</UI>. Use the buttons on its row:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| <UI>Edit</UI> | Opens the same form you used to create it. |
| <UI>View</UI> | Opens the public page. |
| <UI>Delete</UI> | Asks you to confirm, then removes the item. |
| <UI>More actions</UI> | Opens the menu below. |

The <UI>More actions</UI> menu offers:

- <UI>View Analytics</UI> for courses. See [Create a course](/create/create-a-course).
- <UI>Manage Enrollment</UI> for events, courses and quests, or <UI>Manage Applications</UI> for jobs. This is where you approve people and review who joined.
- <UI>Hide from profile</UI>, which removes the item from your public lists, Discover and search without deleting it. Choose <UI>Unhide</UI> to bring it back. An event can be hidden once it has finished, a quest once it has ended, and a course with an end date once that date has passed.
- <UI>Notifications on</UI> or <UI>Notifications off</UI>, which turns notifications about that event, course, job or content item on or off for you.

<Figure
  src="/media/create/your-dashboard/item-actions.webp"
  alt="An event row on the dashboard with the More actions menu open, showing Manage Enrollment, Hide from profile and Notifications on."
  caption="The row buttons and the More actions menu."
/>

### Items that have started are locked

Once an event or a quest starts, you can no longer edit or delete it, and a lock icon appears next to its title. Ended quests are read-only. Plan changes before the start time.

## Check your metaverse time and quest credits

Two panels sit beside the tabs.

- <UI>Metaverse time</UI> shows minutes <UI>scheduled</UI> and <UI>leftover</UI> for events in shared 3D worlds. See [Host in a 3D world](/create/host-in-a-world).
- <UI>Quest credits</UI> shows how many <UI>7 day</UI> and <UI>30 day</UI> credits you have left and how many <UI>active quests</UI> you are running. See [Create a quest](/create/create-a-quest).

Owners and admins also see <UI>Buy time</UI> and <UI>Buy credits</UI> buttons. Moderators can use what is in the balance but cannot buy more.

## Open your tools

The cards above the tabs open the rest of your tools. Which cards you see depends on your plan and your role.

| Card | What it opens | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| <UI>Account Settings</UI> | Your settings | Your own dashboard |
| <UI>Wallets</UI> | Your Hive3 wallet and linked wallets | Your own dashboard |
| <UI>Accounts you manage</UI> | Communities where you are a staff member | Your own dashboard |
| <UI>API Keys</UI> | Developer keys. See [Authentication and API keys](/developers/api-authentication). | Your own dashboard, Honey Bee and up |
| <UI>Account Builder</UI> | Your public page. See [Build your account page](/create/build-your-page). | Everyone |
| <UI>Permissions</UI> | Staff roles. See [Roles and permissions](/community/roles-and-permissions). | Owners and admins, Honey Bee and up |
| <UI>Chat Settings</UI> | [Discord and Telegram](/community/chat-integrations) | Honey Bee and up |
| <UI>Broadcasts</UI> | [Email broadcasts](/community/broadcasts) | Honey Bee and up |
| <UI>Monetize</UI> | Payments, subscriptions and tickets. See [Get paid](/community/get-paid). | Owners and admins, Honey Bee and up |
| <UI>AI</UI> | The [AI assistant](/community/ai-assistant) | Honey Bee and up |
| <UI>Worlds</UI> | Book and enter 3D worlds | Honey Bee and up |
| <UI>Streaming</UI> | [Go live](/create/go-live) | Everyone |
| <UI>Affiliates</UI> | [Affiliates](/community/affiliates) | Everyone |
| <UI>Add Ons</UI> | [Add-ons](/create/create-an-add-on) | Everyone |
| <UI>Members</UI> | [Members and applications](/community/members-and-applications). A red badge shows applications waiting. | Everyone |

<Callout type="tip">
Worker Bee accounts publish everything publicly. When you need members-only content, subscriptions, tickets or a private community, compare [plans](/start/plans).
</Callout>
