Hive3 events can happen inside a 3D world where people walk around, meet and talk. You book the time, publish the event as usual, and then enter the world as its host. This guide covers the two kinds of world, how booking and metaverse time work, how to get in, and how to manage people you have banned.

**Who can do this:** [Honey Bee and above](/start/plans). Worker Bee accounts cannot book a world. Owners, admins and moderators of an account can book and host. Buying metaverse time and choosing a Royal Hive world's design are limited to owners and admins.

## Shared worlds and your own world

| | Honey Bee | Swarm | Royal Hive |
|---|---|---|---|
| Where your events run | Any shared world, chosen for each event | Your default shared world | Your own world |
| Most people per event | 500 | 500 | 100 |
| Event time | Pay as you go | 1 hour a month included, then pay as you go | Unlimited |

- A **shared world** is a Hive3 venue that many accounts book in turn, such as the Hive3 Dome or the Night Lounge. It holds up to 500 people. A shared world hosts one event at a time, so each booking holds the world for its time slot.
- A **Royal Hive world** is reserved for your account. Its events, link and settings stay the same. It holds up to 100 people. Its time is unlimited, but the 100-person limit still applies.

## Choose your world

### Honey Bee

You choose a world each time you create an event. <UI>Book an hour</UI> on the page opens the event form with <UI>Metaverse Event</UI> already selected.

### Swarm

Your events run in your default shared world, marked <UI>Default</UI>. To change it, select <UI>Set as default</UI> on another world under <UI>All worlds</UI>. If you have upcoming events in other worlds, Hive3 asks whether to make the new world your default, and offers <UI>Keep them where they are</UI> or <UI>Move them to</UI> the new world. Events that clash with an existing booking in the new world stay where they are, and Hive3 tells you how many.

### Royal Hive

Your world starts closed and you pick its design.

<Steps>
<Step title="Pick a design">
Under <UI>All worlds</UI>, select <UI>Make this my world</UI> on the design you want. Later, select <UI>Use for my world</UI> on another design to switch. The world you are using shows <UI>Your world's design</UI>. You cannot switch while the world is hosting an event, and Hive3 asks you to wait until it ends.
</Step>
<Step title="Decide who can enter">
In <UI>Current world</UI>, under <UI>World admission</UI>, choose <UI>Closed</UI>, <UI>Community members only</UI> or <UI>Anyone with a Hive3 account</UI>. A new world is closed.
</Step>
<Step title="Share the link">
Select <UI>Copy link</UI> to copy your world's address.
</Step>
</Steps>

World admission applies whenever no event is running. While one of your events is on, the event's own rules decide who can enter: people need an RSVP or approval, and a subscription or ticket if the event asks for one.

## Book an event in a world

<Steps>
<Step title="Start the event form">
Select <UI>Book an hour</UI> (Honey Bee), the button named after your world, such as <UI>Book Hive3 Dome</UI> (Swarm), or <UI>Book an event in your world</UI> (Royal Hive) on the Worlds page. Or start from <UI>New Event</UI> on your dashboard and choose <UI>Metaverse Event</UI>. See [Create an event](/create/create-an-event).
</Step>
<Step title="Set the capacity and the world">
In <UI>Event Type</UI>, enter <UI>Maximum participants</UI> and choose the world. The maximum is 500 in a shared world and 100 in a Royal Hive world.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose the time">
In <UI>Scheduling</UI>, drag across the time you need. Slots other events already hold in that world show as <UI>Booked</UI>.
</Step>
<Step title="Publish">
Create the event. Attendees RSVP the usual way.
</Step>
</Steps>

If a Royal Hive account has not chosen a design yet, the event form shows <UI>Set up your own world</UI> with a <UI>Choose your world</UI> button. It opens your Worlds page in a new tab, so you keep your place in the form.

## Keep track of metaverse time

Events in a shared world use metaverse time, counted in the minutes you book. Royal Hive worlds do not use it.

- **Swarm** includes 1 hour in each month. Included time does not roll over. Time is counted against the month the event starts in.
- **Honey Bee** has no included time. You buy 1-hour packs with <UI>Buy time</UI>. Each pack lasts 30 days from purchase.
- When you book, Hive3 uses included time first and then your packs, starting with the pack that expires soonest.

The <UI>Metaverse time</UI> card on the dashboard and on the Worlds page shows <UI>scheduled</UI> and <UI>leftover</UI> minutes. When you schedule an event, a line above the calendar shows how much time you have left this month. If the event is too long, it says how many minutes you are over, and owners and admins can select <UI>Top up</UI>. The <UI>Top up metaverse time</UI> window shows your balance, offers a recommended purchase sized to the shortfall, and sells the <UI>1 Hour</UI> pack. Prices are on [Plans and pricing](/start/plans).

If you delete an event before it starts, the purchased time it used is returned to your packs. Once the event has started, its time is used.

<Callout type="note">
Moderators can book events with the time available but cannot buy more. Ask an owner or admin to top up.
</Callout>

## Enter as the host

Owners, admins and moderators of the account that owns the event can enter its world at any time until the event ends. Guests can only enter from 15 minutes before the start.

<Path items={["Event page", "Enter as host"]} />

Open the event and select <UI>Enter as host</UI>. You can also select it on the <UI>Manage Enrollment</UI> page. From the Worlds page, <UI>Enter world</UI> (Royal Hive) or the button named after your world, such as <UI>Enter Hive3 Dome</UI> (Swarm), opens the world itself, and <UI>Enter</UI> on any card under <UI>All worlds</UI> does the same. The Worlds page opens the world in a new tab.

If it does not open straight away:

- A Royal Hive world can need a moment to start. If you see <UI>Your world is starting up. Try again in a moment.</UI>, wait a few seconds and try again.
- Hive3 shows <UI>Private worlds are at capacity right now. Try again in a few minutes.</UI> when all private worlds are busy.
- During a booked event a shared world is held for that event. Anyone else who tries to enter sees that it is reserved for the event.

## What guests need to enter

Guests open the event page and select <UI>Enter world</UI>. They need to:

- have an accepted RSVP (or approval, if you chose <UI>Apply for entry</UI>);
- meet the event's access rules, such as membership, a subscription or a ticket;
- arrive no more than 15 minutes before the start, and before the end; and
- not be banned from the world.

People who are not yet members of your community are asked to join or apply first.

## Manage world bans

Hosts can ban someone from inside the world. A banned person cannot enter that world again and sees <UI>You've been banned from this world.</UI> Bans are separate for each world. Owners, admins and moderators of the account cannot be banned from its own world.

Bans from your own world appear under <UI>World bans</UI> on the Worlds page, with each person's name and handle, who banned them and when. Select <UI>Unban</UI> next to a name and confirm to let them back in. Bans in a shared world are handled by the Hive3 team.

## Tips

- Book the time before you promote the event. A shared world holds one event at a time, so popular slots go first.
- Keep your event capacity at or below the number of people you expect. It cannot go above 500, or 100 in a Royal Hive world.
- For a Royal Hive world, leave admission on <UI>Closed</UI> between events and let the event's own rules decide who joins while it runs.
- Enter a few minutes early to check the space and greet people as they arrive.
