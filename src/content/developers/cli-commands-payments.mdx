These commands arrived in CLI version 0.5, so every command and flag on this page is **(0.5+)** unless it is marked **(0.7+)**; `hive3 tickets list`, `get`, `update` and `mine` existed in 0.4 but now follow the rules below. If `hive3 products` prints `Unknown command: products`, your installed CLI is older; run `hive3 update --check`. Every command prints JSON. Shared conventions such as output and exit codes are on the main [CLI command reference](/developers/cli-commands#how-to-read-this-reference).

`products`, `subscriptions`, `stripe` and `payments` need `payments:read` or `payments:write`; `tickets` needs `tickets:read` or `tickets:write`. Keys from before 0.5 do not have the payment scopes: run `hive3 login` again. For an account you run, `--account` also needs `communities:read` or `communities:write`, the key's account allowlist, and your current **owner or admin** role there; moderators cannot manage payments. The API side is on [Products, subscription plans and Stripe](/developers/api-selling), [Tickets](/developers/api-tickets) and [Payments](/developers/api-payments).

## Safety rules for every write

- **`--yes` is required** on every command that changes something. Without it the command stops before calling the API.
- **Idempotency.** Seller writes accept `--idempotency-key <key>` (1 to 128 characters); without it the CLI generates one. Keep the key to repeat the same request safely after a lost response. Payment operations use `--request-id` instead.
- **No automatic retries.** Steps that reach the payment provider are tried once. If one failed or you are not sure it ran, check the item, `hive3 stripe status` or `hive3 payments` history before trying again with a new key.
- **Starting sales** (creating a product, plan or ticket, or turning one back on) needs a claimed seller account on Honey Bee or above with a ready payment provider. Stopping sales always works.
- **Currencies** are `USD` (default), `EUR` and `GBP`.

## Products

| Command | What it does | Flags | Scopes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hive3 products list` | Lists the account's products. | `--account` | `payments:read` |
| `hive3 products get <id>` | Reads one product. | None | `payments:read` |
| `hive3 products create` | Creates a product. | `--title <title>` (required), `--description <text>`, `--image-url <url>`, `--account`, `--file <path>`, `--idempotency-key <key>`, `--yes` | `payments:write` |
| `hive3 products delete <id>` | Removes the product from future sales. Existing subscriptions, purchases and refunds are kept. | `--idempotency-key`, `--yes` | `payments:write` |

## Subscription plans

`hive3 subscriptions` manages the plans you sell. Your own subscriptions as a buyer are under [`hive3 payments`](#payments).

| Command | What it does | Flags | Scopes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hive3 subscriptions list` | Lists the plans of a product. | `--product <product-id>` (required) | `payments:read` |
| `hive3 subscriptions get <plan-id>` | Reads one plan. | None | `payments:read` |
| `hive3 subscriptions create` | Creates a plan and starts selling it. | `--product <product-id>`, `--name <name>`, `--amount <decimal>`, `--interval monthly\|yearly` (all required), `--currency <code>`, `--description <text>`, `--file`, `--idempotency-key`, `--yes` | `payments:write` |
| `hive3 subscriptions update <plan-id>` | Replaces a plan's details. | `--name`, `--amount`, `--currency`, `--interval` (all required), `--description`, `--file`, `--idempotency-key`, `--yes` | `payments:write` |
| `hive3 subscriptions activate <plan-id>`, `deactivate <plan-id>` | Starts or stops new sales of a plan. | `--idempotency-key`, `--yes` | `payments:write` |
| `hive3 subscriptions delete <plan-id>` | Removes a plan from future sales. Existing subscriptions are kept. | `--idempotency-key`, `--yes` | `payments:write` |

A plan connected to PayPal cannot change its price, currency or interval; create a replacement plan instead. Its name and description can still change.

## Tickets

Tickets are the one-time purchases on an account's events, courses, quests and bookings.

| Command | What it does | Flags | Scopes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hive3 tickets list` | Lists tickets an account sells. | `--account`, `--limit <1-100>`, `--cursor <cursor>` | `tickets:read` |
| `hive3 tickets entities` | Lists the events, courses, quests and bookings a ticket can be attached to, with their IDs. | `--account` | `tickets:read` |
| `hive3 tickets get <id>` | Reads one ticket. | None | `tickets:read` |
| `hive3 tickets mine` | Lists tickets you bought, with their status. | `--limit`, `--cursor` | `tickets:read` |
| `hive3 tickets create` | Creates a ticket and attaches it. | `--product <id>`, `--name <name>`, `--amount <decimal>`, `--entity-type event\|course\|quest\|coordination`, `--entity-id <id>` (all required), `--currency <code>`, `--description <text>`, `--status active\|inactive`, `--file`, `--idempotency-key`, `--yes` | `tickets:write` |
| `hive3 tickets update <id>` | Changes a ticket. At least one field is required. | `--name`, `--description`, `--amount`, `--currency`, `--status`, `--file`, `--idempotency-key`, `--yes` | `tickets:write` |
| `hive3 tickets attach <id>` | Moves a ticket to another item of the same account. | `--entity-type`, `--entity-id` (both required), `--file`, `--idempotency-key`, `--yes` | `tickets:write` |
| `hive3 tickets remove <id>` | Stops selling the ticket (sets it to `inactive`). Buyers keep their access. | `--idempotency-key`, `--yes` | `tickets:write` |

- `--amount` is a positive decimal with at most two decimals, such as `12.50`, up to `1000000`.
- The product and the item must belong to the same account. Once anyone has checked out with a ticket, it cannot be moved to another item.
- `create`, `update` and `attach` accept `--file <path>` with a JSON object instead of field flags (`productId`, `name`, `amount`, `currency`, `description`, `entityType`, `entityId`, `status`); flags override the file.

```bash title="Sell tickets for an event"
hive3 products create --title "Event tickets" --account design-guild --yes
hive3 tickets entities --account design-guild
hive3 tickets create --product "<product-id>" --name "Entry ticket" --amount 25.00 --entity-type event --entity-id "<event-id>" --yes
hive3 tickets update "<ticket-id>" --amount 30.00 --yes
hive3 tickets remove "<ticket-id>" --yes
```

## Stripe

`hive3 stripe` manages the account's Stripe connection. Owners and admins only.

| Command | What it does | Flags | Scopes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hive3 stripe status` | Shows whether the account is connected and what Stripe still needs, and prints the owner account ID used by `disconnect`. | `--account` | `payments:read` |
| `hive3 stripe onboard` | Starts or resumes Stripe onboarding and returns a private link. | `--account`, `--yes` | `payments:write` |
| `hive3 stripe refresh` | Asks Stripe for the latest status. | `--account` | `payments:write` |
| `hive3 stripe dashboard` | Returns a private sign-in link to the Stripe dashboard. | `--account`, `--yes` | `payments:write` |
| `hive3 stripe tax-setup` | Returns a link to the tax settings on the account's Hive3 dashboard. | `--account`, `--yes` | `payments:write` |
| `hive3 stripe disconnect` | Disconnects Stripe from Hive3. | `--account`, `--yes`, `--confirm <owner-account-id>` (required) | `payments:write` |

Identity checks, bank details and Stripe's terms are completed by the account owner on Stripe's pages in the browser; the CLI cannot do them. Onboarding and dashboard links give access to the Stripe account: open them privately and never share or save them.

Disconnecting stops renewing Stripe subscriptions at the end of their period, turns off the account's Stripe plans and removes the connection from Hive3; the Stripe account itself stays open. New Stripe sales are blocked from the moment it starts. If it is interrupted, run the same command again to finish.

## Payments

`hive3 payments` reads histories and runs buyer and seller payment actions. Every action is previewed first, then confirmed with the exact text the preview printed.

| Command | What it does | Scopes |
|---|---|---|
| `hive3 payments history` | Your payments. `--limit`, `--cursor`. | `payments:read` |
| `hive3 payments subscriptions` | Your subscriptions, with the IDs used below. | `payments:read` |
| `hive3 payments tickets`, `refunds`, `sales` | Ticket purchases, refund requests or sales. `--account <seller>` for an account you run, `--limit`, `--cursor`. | `payments:read` |
| `hive3 payments quest-credits`, `metaverse-time` | Quest credit or metaverse time purchases. `--account`, `--limit`, `--cursor`. | `payments:read` |
| `hive3 payments preview <action>` | Shows what an action will do and prints its `confirm` text. Changes nothing. | `payments:write` |
| `hive3 payments <action> --yes --confirm "<confirm>" --request-id <id>` | Runs the action. | `payments:write` |

| Action | Arguments |
|---|---|
| `checkout subscription <plan-id>`, `checkout ticket <ticket-id>` | `--provider stripe\|paypal` |
| `checkout membership` | `--tier honey\|swarm\|royal` (required), `--billing month\|quarter\|year` (0.7+; default `month`), `--account <owned-account>` |
| `checkout quest-credit <account>` | `--plan-type single\|bundle` (required) |
| `checkout metaverse-time <account>` | `--quantity <1-100 hours>` (required) |
| `finalize stripe <session-id>` | `--kind membership\|subscription\|ticket\|questCredit\|metaverseTime` (required) |
| `finalize subscription <plan-id>` | `--checkout-intent <id>`, `--paypal-subscription <id>` (both required) |
| `finalize ticket <ticket-id>` | `--order <order-id>` (required) |
| `subscription cancel\|resume\|portal <subscription-id>` | None |
| `subscription revise <subscription-id>` | `--plan <new-plan-id>` (required; PayPal subscriptions only) |
| `membership cancel\|resume\|portal` | None |
| `membership revise` | `--tier honey\|swarm\|royal` (required), `--billing month\|quarter\|year` (0.7+; keeps the current period when left out); an upgrade charges your saved payment method immediately |
| `refund request <purchased-ticket-id>` | `--reason <text>` |
| `refund approve\|reject <refund-request-id>` | `--note <text>`; owners and admins of the seller only |

`--billing` (0.7+) picks how often a Hive3 membership is billed: `month` (full price), `quarter` (10% off, paid up front every 3 months) or `year` (20% off, paid up front once a year). Another value stops with `--billing must be month, quarter or year`. The preview's amount names the price for the whole period and the period, for example `720.00 USD yearly honey IMMEDIATE PRORATED INVOICE`. With `membership revise`, a higher tier or a longer period starts now and a lower tier or a shorter period starts at the next renewal (`SCHEDULE NEXT BILLING PERIOD`); a higher tier always starts now. Revising to your current tier and period while a change is scheduled cancels that change. See [Billing periods](/start/plans#billing-periods).

```bash title="Switch your membership to yearly billing"
hive3 payments preview membership revise --tier honey --billing year
hive3 payments membership revise --tier honey --billing year --yes \
  --confirm "<confirm text from the preview>" --request-id honey-yearly-001
```

```bash title="Buy a ticket"
hive3 payments preview checkout ticket "<ticket-id>" --provider stripe
hive3 payments checkout ticket "<ticket-id>" --provider stripe --yes \
  --confirm "<confirm text from the preview>" --request-id buy-ticket-001
```

- **Paste `confirm` unchanged.** It names the account, provider, action, target and amount. If anything changed since the preview, the command stops with `confirm must exactly match the current payment preview`; run the preview again.
- **New purchases are paid in the browser.** Checkouts, portals and PayPal plan changes return a private link that the buyer opens in their own browser to pay or approve. The CLI never starts a new purchase on its own.
- **Some confirmed actions take effect at once.** `membership revise` to a higher tier charges the saved payment method on your membership straight away for the prorated difference, and a move to a longer billing period charges the new period today less a credit for the unused time (a failed payment leaves the plan unchanged; a downgrade or shorter period waits for the next renewal). `refund approve` sends the refund through the payment provider. Run these only when the person asked for that exact change.
- **One attempt per request ID.** Use a new `--request-id` (up to 128 characters) for each action, and the same one only to repeat the identical request. If the result is unknown, check `hive3 payments history` or the browser before doing anything else; a second checkout for the same thing is blocked.
- **Not supported:** changing the plan of a Stripe creator subscription (its portal handles payment methods, invoices and cancelling), managing an older PayPal Hive3 membership, payouts and wallet transfers. A membership checkout for an account you own is for its owner only; after that, that account manages its membership with its own sign-in.
